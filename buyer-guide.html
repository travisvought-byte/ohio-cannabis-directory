Your dispensary shopping plan

A first-visit guide and shopping worksheet. Start with your goal and experience, then discuss product, strength and budget with dispensary staff. Your answers stay on this page and are not saved or sent.

What are you trying to accomplish?

How long will my purchase last?

Use the same unit for package quantity and amount per use: pieces for gummies, mL for oil, grams for flower or concentrate. These are shopping estimates, not dose recommendations. Leave blank if you do not yet know your amount.

Enter your own estimates to calculate supply duration.

Example: 10 gummies, one-quarter gummy per use, twice per week = 40 uses or 20 weeks. This says nothing about the appropriate THC amount. Check mg THC per gummy and whether it can be portioned reliably. Buy a small trial amount first when your response is unknown.

Understand THC and CBD on the label

Calculate labeled content in an amount you enter. This does not recommend an amount, predict effects or calculate absorbed THC. Use the label basis exactly as printed; do not substitute THCA for total THC.

Enter labeled THC and your amount to calculate labeled content.

Examples: 10 mg per gummy x 0.25 gummy = 2.5 mg labeled THC. 20% x 0.1 grams x 10 = 20 mg labeled THC in the material, which does not establish what you absorb. Dividing a product may not distribute cannabinoids evenly.

Take a plan with you, then record your experience

The two-page visit companion has a one-page handoff starting with your goal, budget, preferences and label questions, followed by a personal experience journal. Print page 1 for the visit and page 2 for later notes.

Record product and batch, form, labeled content, amount used, onset, duration, helpful and unwanted effects, and questions for your next visit. Keep notes private; they do not establish a safe dose or fitness to drive.

Ask before buying

  • How much THC and CBD are in one serving and the whole package?
  • How is this used, and when do effects begin, peak and wear off?
  • Can it be measured or divided reliably? What device is required?
  • What is the final cost with taxes, fees and any device?
  • Are there allergens, added ingredients or a batch testing report?
  • How should I store it securely, and what is the expiration date?

For new or occasional edible users, Health Canada gives a starting reference of 2.5 mg THC or less and advises allowing up to four hours for full effects before taking more. Higher-potency extracts can be difficult for beginners to measure. Oral oils used under the tongue and swallowed oils can behave differently. Ask a clinician or pharmacist about medicines and health concerns.

Do not drive while impaired. Keep products locked away from children and pets. Avoid combining with alcohol. Strain names do not guarantee effects.

For Ohio adult-use purchases, bring current government-issued photo ID showing you are 21 or older. Medical purchasers should bring their active registry card and linked ID and have an active recommendation. Confirm store payment options and hours before travel.
