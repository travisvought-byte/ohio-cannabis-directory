🏆 The Silver Cup

Sail Pillage the Reefer for real silver. The three best captains of MJBizCon week win silver half dollars: ten for first, five for second and one for third. It’s free to enter, and every run is replayed move by move before its score counts.

Opens at 12:00 AM Pacific on Tuesday, December 1, 2026

⛵ Play and enter

Run a cannabis business or a group that helps people? Add your business to your state’s directory on The Reefer. Listing is free.

The prizes

  1. 1st place: 10 silver half dollars, about 2.548 oz of silver, worth about $105.44.
  2. 2nd place: 5 silver half dollars, about 1.381 oz of silver, worth about $57.14.
  3. 3rd place: 1 silver half dollar, worth $6.12 to $14.97.

Values are approximate, at the silver spot price of $41.38 a troy ounce (Kitco, bid price at 2:11 PM Eastern on November 26, 2026), and they move with the price of silver. They assume first place gets five 90% and five 40% halves and second place three 90% and two 40%. The sponsor picks the coins: every one is at least 40% silver, and about half of them are 90%. All three prizes together come to about $168.70 to $177.55. The details are in the official rules.

When

  • Opens: 12:00 AM Pacific on Tuesday, December 1, 2026. The boards start from zero then, and nothing played before counts.
  • Closes: 11:59 PM Pacific on Friday, December 4, 2026, MJBizCon’s last day. Runs still at sea end then and are sent one last time.
  • Practice: until it opens, you can sail practice runs. They’re checked the same way and shown on a practice board, and they never count toward the prizes.

How to enter

  1. Open Pillage the Reefer on a phone or a computer and tap Enter the Silver Cup on the title screen.
  2. Pick a captain name for the boards and give an email so you can be reached if you win. Add a crew or business name if you like.
  3. Confirm you’re 21 or older, agree to the official rules, and set sail.
  4. Play. Your run sends itself as you go: every few minutes, whenever you dock, and when you leave the page. To send it right away, tap 🏆 Silver Cup under the map, then Send my run now.

Start as many runs as you like, up to 60 a day. Only your best checked run counts, and your captain name stays yours on every run you start with the same email.

How scoring works

  • Your score is your renown: the renown your captain earns in one Cup run, the same number the game shows in your captain’s log. Trade at a profit, win fights, finish jobs, find treasure and see new ports to earn it.
  • Every run is replayed: the game records each move you make, and the Cup’s server plays those moves back in its own copy of the game and works out the score itself. A score sent from a phone is never taken on trust, and a run whose replay doesn’t match is turned away.
  • Rough seas: Cup runs sail in real danger. Pirates and warships hit harder and spot you from further off, and there are no gentle seas. If your ship sinks, the run is over, and the renown it earned still counts.
  • Ties: if two captains tie, the one whose run reached that score first wins.
  • Crews: a crew or business scores its three best captains added up, so a big crew doesn’t win on numbers alone. The crews board is for bragging rights: the silver goes by each captain’s own rank.
  • Help is welcome: friends can coach you or take a turn at the helm of your run. Each person can win one prize, and it goes to the person who entered the run with their own email.

      Breakout leaders

      The boards refresh every minute. Every top run is also looked over by a person before a prize goes out.

      Sail together

      While you sail a Cup or practice run, you see the other Cup captains sailing the same sea live, with their captain name and crew above their ship. Two people playing side by side see each other. They can’t attack, trade with or block you, and they never change your score. Turn it off any time in Settings, and others won’t see you either.

      Fair play

      • One game for everyone: the game is frozen for the Cup as version cup1. From December 1 to 4, the only changes allowed are fixes that can’t change a score, and each one is listed below.
      • Out of bounds: changing the game or its code, scripts or bots that play for you, tampering with what your run sends, or entering under several names to win more than one prize. Any of these can get a captain disqualified.
      • A person checks the top: before the silver goes out, the top runs are looked over by hand.

      • None yet.

      Winners and prizes

      After the Cup closes, the top runs are checked, and winners are contacted at the email they entered, within 7 days. Prizes ship within 30 days of a winner sending a US shipping address. Winners are posted here by captain name. Questions about the Cup or a prize: veteranhomeguardians@gmail.com.

      What’s sent, and who sees it

      Entering sends your captain name, crew and email to the Cup’s server, and a Cup run sends its recorded moves so it can be replayed. Your captain name, crew and scores go on the public boards. Your email never does: it’s used only to reach you about the Cup, and every email is wiped 60 days after the close. Live captains are on unless you turn them off in Settings, and while they’re on, other Cup players see your captain name, crew and ship while you sail.
