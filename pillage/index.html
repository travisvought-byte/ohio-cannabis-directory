Three seas. Eight ports. One very opinionated parrot.

Start a new voyage? Your saved one will be lost.

How to play

  • Sail: tap or click the sea and your crew sails there. On a keyboard, steer with the arrow keys or W A S D.
  • Dock, talk and dig: sail up to a port or a treasure mark and press the button that appears, or Enter.
  • Trade: every port grows one strain cheaply. Sell it somewhere far away, best of all in another sea.
  • Pillage: bump into a fat merchant ship to try boarding her. Blue-sailed Customs cutters will want a word.
  • Explore: sail off the edge of the chart to reach the Florida Straits and the Spanish Main. The sea chart sets a course for anywhere you've found.
  • Win: find three pieces of Captain Featherstone's map and dig up the Emerald Bale. Your captain's log keeps track of every goal.

Fine print

This is a cartoon. Every captain, parrot and strain in it is made up, the ports are real places borrowed for fun, and nothing here is advice about real cannabis or real law. It's meant for adults 21 and over. If you want the real rules where you live, check your state on The Reefer.
