Pillage the Reefer

Five seas. Eleven ports. Pirates, treasure and one very opinionated parrot.

Start a new voyage? Your saved one will be lost.

How to play

  • Sail: tap or click the sea and your crew sails there. On a keyboard, steer with the arrow keys or W A S D.
  • Dock, talk and dig: sail up to a port or a treasure mark and press the button that appears, or Enter.
  • Trade: every port grows one strain cheaply. Sell it somewhere far away, best of all in another sea. Smuggler's Hash, Amber Rosin and Cook's Biscuits are made in one port each and fetch a premium where they're wanted.
  • Plan routes: every market you visit goes in your trade ledger, which suggests the best runs you know. In port, the Routes tab sails you straight to any port you know in one go: the days pass, and you might meet Customs, pirates or a surprise on the way.
  • Smoke a bale: every strain gives its own short boost, like faster sails, sharper haggling, steadier aim or better luck. Smoke one from the market or your captain's log. It lasts about two days of sailing.
  • Fight: bump into a fat merchant ship to attack her. Black-sailed pirates will come for you. Fire broadsides to wear them down, then board them for their loot, or run if they look too strong. Your parrot rates every fight before it starts. Blue-sailed Customs cutters just want a word. If you're being chased, you can burn a bale: it's a coin flip whether they get high and wander off.
  • Odds: every risky choice shows its real chance, and that's exactly the chance rolled. Your ship, skills and boosts set the odds, and each miss in a row gives you a second wind of +10% on your next check, up to +30%.
  • Level up: trading at a profit, winning fights, finishing requests, finding treasure and seeing new ports all earn renown. Each level gives a skill point for Fighting, Sailing or Charm. Shipwrights sell a bigger hold, a stouter hull, cannons, boarders and faster sails.
  • Losing isn't the end: a pirate who beats you takes part of your cargo and a little gold, never your ship, upgrades or level. Any port can patch your hull.
  • Explore: sail off the edge of the chart to reach the Florida Straits, the Gulf of Mexico, the Spanish Main and the Lesser Antilles. Expect surprises on the way, and buy, win or stumble on treasure maps. The sea chart sets a course for anywhere you've found.
  • Win: find three pieces of Captain Featherstone's map and dig up the Emerald Bale, beat the pirate Captain Silas Gristle (he turns up on the Spanish Main at level 7 and waits for you to come to him) and reach level 10. Your captain's log keeps track of every goal.
