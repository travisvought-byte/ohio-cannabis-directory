Pillage the Reefer

Captain a leaky sloop across a cartoon Caribbean. Buy bales cheap where they grow, sell them dear where they don't, fire cannonballs (or Brick Weed) at pirates, smuggle contraband past Crown Customs, level up your captain and chat with the strange folk of Tortuga, Havana, Veracruz, Barbados and beyond. Lose a sea battle and your ship goes down, so pick your fights. Somewhere out there, a retired captain buried the finest crop that ever sailed.

How to play

  • Sail: tap or click the sea and your crew sails there. On a keyboard, steer with the arrow keys or W A S D.
  • Dock, talk and dig: sail up to a port or a treasure mark and press the button that appears, or Enter.
  • Name your captain: pick a name, a ship's name, sail colours and a flag. More colours and flags are earned along the way and stay earned for every voyage. Change them any time from Settings.
  • Trade: every port grows one strain cheaply. Sell it somewhere far away, best of all in another sea. Smuggler's Hash, Amber Rosin and Cook's Biscuits are made in one port each and fetch a premium where they're wanted.
  • Markets run out: each market keeps only a few bales on the shelf and restocks over a few days. Every bale you buy nudges the price up and every bale you sell nudges it down, so spread your trade around.
  • Plan routes: every market you visit goes in your trade ledger, which suggests the best runs you know. In port, the Routes tab sails you straight to any port you know in one go. The days pass, and the longer the trip and the richer your hold, the likelier you meet pirates on the way.
  • Smoke a bale: every strain gives its own short boost, like faster sails, sharper haggling, steadier aim or better luck. Smoke one from the market or your captain's log. It lasts about two days of sailing.
  • Fight: bump into a fat merchant ship to attack her. Black-sailed pirates will come for you, from little sloops to man-o'-wars far stronger than you. Fire cannonballs (buy more in any market) or lob bales of weed: cheap Brick Weed is made for it. Weed hits softer, but every bale that lands gets their crew higher, so they aim worse and are easier to board. Your parrot rates every fight before it starts.
  • Roll the die: every risky choice rolls a twenty-sided die and shows the number you need, like 11 or more for a 50% chance. A natural 20 earns extra renown, doubles a shot and fills a boarding party's pockets. Each miss in a row gives you a second wind of +10% on your next roll, up to +30%.
  • Real danger: a ship sunk in battle ends the voyage. When a fight looks bad, run for it, pay the pirates off or strike your colours (they take your open hold and a third of your gold, and you sail on). If you'd rather not risk it, pick gentle seas when you start a voyage.
  • Smuggle: a shipwright can build a smuggler's hold that hides your most valuable bales from Customs and pirates. Kraken Kush, Mermaid's Kiss and Black Pearl are contraband: no market sells them, and Customs take any they can see.
  • Keep a hideout: old Grandpère Toussaint in Petit-Goâve will sell you the key to a cave on Île-à-Vache. Gold and bales you leave there survive a sinking, ready for your next voyage.
  • Level up: trading at a profit, winning fights, finishing requests, finding treasure and seeing new ports all earn renown. Each level gives a skill point for Fighting, Sailing or Charm. Shipwrights sell a bigger hold, a stouter hull, cannons, boarders, faster sails and a smuggler's hold.
  • Goals and relics: your captain's log lists goals, and many earn a relic, like a spyglass that lifts the fog faster or a lucky doubloon. Relics go down with the ship. The hall of fame remembers your best captains.
  • Explore: sail off the edge of the chart to reach the Florida Straits, the Gulf of Mexico, the Spanish Main and the Lesser Antilles. Expect surprises on the way, and buy, win or stumble on treasure maps. The sea chart sets a course for anywhere you've found.
  • Win: find three pieces of Captain Featherstone's map and dig up the Emerald Bale, beat the pirate Captain Silas Gristle (he turns up on the Spanish Main at level 7 and waits for you to come to him) and reach level 10. Your captain's log keeps track of every goal.
