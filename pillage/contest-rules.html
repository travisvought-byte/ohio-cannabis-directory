Silver Cup official rules

No purchase necessary. A purchase won’t improve your chances. Open to adults 21 and over in the United States. Void where prohibited.

Run a cannabis business or a group that helps people? Add your business to your state’s directory on The Reefer. Listing is free and has nothing to do with the contest.

1. Who runs it

The Silver Cup is run by The Reefer (the-reefer.com), which Travis Vought makes and maintains, called “the sponsor” here. Questions: veteranhomeguardians@gmail.com. The Silver Cup isn’t run, sponsored or endorsed by MJBizCon or its organizers, or by Facebook or any other site where it’s shared, and none of them is responsible for it. By entering, you release Facebook (Meta) and those other sites from any claim about the Silver Cup.

2. No purchase necessary

Entering and playing are free. Pillage the Reefer has nothing for sale, and nothing you could buy anywhere would help your score.

3. Who can enter

  • You must be 21 or older when you enter, and live in the United States (the 50 states and the District of Columbia).
  • The contest is void where prohibited or restricted by law. You’re responsible for making sure you can take part where you live.
  • Travis Vought and the people in his household can’t win a prize.
  • The sponsor may ask a winner to show they’re 21 or older before a prize ships.

4. When

  • The contest runs from 12:00:00 AM Pacific on Tuesday, December 1, 2026 to 11:59:59 PM Pacific on Friday, December 4, 2026. The sponsor’s server clock is the official clock.
  • Scores start from zero when the contest opens. Practice runs and anything played before then don’t count.
  • Runs still at sea when it closes end at the close. Moves sent within five minutes after the close count up to the close, and nothing sent later counts.

5. How to enter

  • Open Pillage the Reefer at the-reefer.com/pillage, choose Enter the Silver Cup, and give a captain name, your own email, and a crew or business name if you like. Confirm you’re 21 or older and agree to these rules.
  • Your email makes you the entrant, and it’s how you’ll be reached if you win. Use your own email, and only one.
  • A captain name belongs to the email that first takes it. The sponsor may refuse or change a captain name or crew name that is offensive, misleading or someone else’s.
  • You can start up to 60 runs a day. The sponsor may limit entries from one network or device if they look automated.

6. How scoring works

  • The Silver Cup is a contest of skill. The game has dice rolls and chance events, as every voyage does, but how far a captain gets depends on how they trade, sail and fight. Each run’s dice are set by the sponsor’s server when the run starts.
  • Your score is the renown your captain earns in a single Cup run. The game records your moves and sends them as you play. The sponsor’s server replays those moves in its own copy of the contest version of the game and works out the score itself. A run whose replay doesn’t match what was sent doesn’t count.
  • Your best checked run is your score. If a ship sinks, that run ends, and the renown it earned counts.
  • Ties go to the earlier verified run: the captain whose run reached the tied score first, by when the server received it, ranks higher.
  • Prizes go by each captain’s own rank on the captains board. The crews board, which adds up a crew’s three best captains, and the breakout boards are for fun, with no prizes.

7. The game during the contest

  • The game is frozen for the contest as version cup1, and every run is checked against it. Contest runs sail harder seas than regular voyages: pirates and warships hit harder and spot you from further off, and there are no gentle seas.
  • During the contest, the sponsor may make only fixes that can’t change a score, like a broken button or a typo. Each one is listed on the contest page.
  • If something outside anyone’s control stops the contest from running fairly, like a server failure, the sponsor may pause it, extend it or end it early, and award the prizes from the checked runs up to that point. Any change is posted on the contest page.

8. Help from others

  • Help is allowed. Friends and other players can coach you, share tips and take a turn at the helm of your run.
  • One prize per person. A prize goes only to the entrant, the person who entered the run with their own email and claims it. It isn’t split, and it isn’t sent to anyone who helped.

9. Fair play and disqualification

The sponsor may disqualify any captain or run, and take back an unshipped prize, for cheating or trying to cheat, including:

  • changing the game, its code or its saved data, or playing a version other than the one on the-reefer.com;
  • scripts, bots or other automated play;
  • tampering with, forging or replaying what a run sends to the server, or attacking or overloading the server;
  • entering under more than one name or email to win more than one prize;
  • deliberately exploiting a bug to raise a score;
  • giving false entry details, like your age.

Every top run is looked over by a person before a prize is awarded. The sponsor’s decisions on scoring, eligibility and disqualification are final.

10. The prizes

  • 1st place: 10 US silver half dollars.
  • 2nd place: 5 US silver half dollars.
  • 3rd place: 1 US silver half dollar.

Every coin is a circulated US half dollar of at least 40% silver: 90% silver halves (dated 1964 and earlier) or 40% silver halves (dated 1965 to 1970). Across all 16 coins, about half are 90% silver and half are 40% silver. Which coins go to which place, and their dates and condition, are the sponsor’s choice.

11. Winners

  • After the close, the top runs are checked and looked over. The sponsor then contacts each winner at the email they entered, within 7 days.
  • A winner has 14 days to reply with a US shipping address. If a winner doesn’t reply in time, can’t be reached, or isn’t eligible, the prize goes to the next eligible captain.
  • Prizes ship within 30 days of a winner’s reply with a shipping address.
  • Winners are posted on the contest page by captain name only.

12. Your information

Entering sends your captain name, your crew or business name and your email to the sponsor’s server, along with your runs’ recorded moves. Captain names, crews and scores are shown on the public boards. Your email is never shown or shared. It’s used only to contact you about the Silver Cup, and every email is wiped from the server 60 days after the close, by February 3, 2027. Live captains are on unless you turn them off in Settings: while they’re on, other Cup players see your captain name, crew and ship while you sail. See Privacy.

13. The rest

  • By entering, you agree to these rules and to the sponsor’s decisions.
  • The sponsor isn’t responsible for lost or late sends, dropped connections, phones or browsers that fail, or other technical problems outside its control.
  • The sponsor may correct a typo or a mistake in these rules. Any change is dated here.
