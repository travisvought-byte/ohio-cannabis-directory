Who lobbied Ohio’s cannabis bills

Want to know who is working on Ohio’s cannabis bills besides the people who testified? This page lists every employer whose registered lobbyists reported SB 56, HB 160 or HB 611, with the agents and reporting periods, and each agent’s federal political contributions that can be tied to the registration. Each entry links to the official filing.

SB 56 HB 160 HB 611 Lobbyists’ federal contributions OHCANN profile and member tracker Committee testimony and lawmakers
How this page is built
  • Every organization and every side is shown in the same format.
  • People appear only in the public role an official record gives them: committee witness, registered lobbying agent, or an officer, director or employee listed on a Form 990. No home addresses, personal contact details or private information.
  • Witnesses who testified for themselves (organization listed as “Self” or left blank) are not named here; the official document carries the name.
  • Quotes are verbatim from the witness’s or organization’s own document and are the first clear statement of position found there. Open the document for the full text.
  • A lobbying activity report shows that an employer lobbied on a bill in a period. It does not show a position.
  • Elected officials’ party, votes and sponsorships are on each lawmaker’s card.
