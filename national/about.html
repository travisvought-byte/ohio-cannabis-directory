About The Reefer

The Reefer is a free cannabis information and resource portal for all 50 states and DC. It grew out of the Ohio Cannabis Directory at OhioWeed.org, which is the template every state is built toward.

How the information is checked

Every answer names its source and the date it was checked. The site holds 333 reviewed answers from 261 sources, as of November 16, 2026. Each answer carries one of three labels:

  • Official source
  • Official source and national compilation
  • National compilation: confirm with the state

A missing answer reads “Not yet reviewed”. It never means something is prohibited. Summaries are not legal advice; the linked source is the authority.

Ground rules

  • No paid placement, paid ranking or paid verification.
  • State laws are presented side by side and factually. The site does not rank states or say which approach is better.
  • Nonpartisan: every party and lawmaker is treated the same way.

Build progress

Ohio is the template. Every state is built toward the same seven-part guide and measured by the same standard. 24 of 51 jurisdictions have all 10 law questions answered; 3 have a state guide. Scorecard as of November 9, 2026.
