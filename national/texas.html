The law in Texas

The same 10 questions are asked of every state. Each answer names its source and the date it was checked.

Is it legal here?

Can adults 21 and older use cannabis without a medical card?

Not permitted No

Texas has no adult-use law. Possessing under 2 ounces of marijuana is a Class B misdemeanor, punishable by jail time and fines. That is how the Senate Research Center described state law in March 2025; national compilations list no adult use as of November 2026.

Tex. Health & Safety Code 481.121, per the Senate Research Center analysis of S.B. 1059 (2025); Ballotpedia; DISA national map · checked November 15, 2026 [1][2][3] Official source and national compilation

Is there a state medical cannabis program?

Limited Low-THC program only

The Department of Public Safety runs the Compassionate Use Program created by the 2015 Texas Compassionate Use Act. DISA’s national map lists Texas as CBD oil only.

Texas Department of Public Safety; DISA national map · checked November 15, 2026 [4][3] Official source and national compilation

Can a past cannabis record be cleared?

Labels name the process the cited state law provides. Whether one record qualifies depends on that record.

By application Expunction or nondisclosure order through the courts

Texas clears records through the courts in two ways. An expunction (Code of Criminal Procedure chapter 55) removes records entirely and is mainly for charges never filed or dismissed, acquittals, pardons and a few listed convictions. An order of nondisclosure (Government Code chapter 411, subchapter E-1) seals an offense from public view after deferred adjudication, or after community supervision for a qualifying misdemeanor or a DWI, following a waiting period of up to five years; a person petitions for it, and law enforcement and some agencies can still see the record. The State Law Library’s summary says its list is not complete and does not mention marijuana.

Tex. Code Crim. Proc. ch. 55 and Tex. Gov’t Code ch. 411, subch. E-1, per the Texas State Law Library (May 20, 2025) · checked November 16, 2026 [5] Official source
