The same 10 questions are asked of every state. Each answer names its source and the date it was checked.

Is it legal here?

Can adults 21 and older use cannabis without a medical card?

Not permitted No

Iowa has no adult-use law. A first conviction for possessing marijuana can bring up to six months in jail, a fine of up to $1,000, or both. Penalties rise for repeat offenses.

Iowa Code 124.401(5) · checked November 15, 2026 [1] Official source

Is there a state medical cannabis program?

Limited Medical cannabidiol card program

The Iowa Department of Health and Human Services registers patients 18 and older who have a health care practitioner’s certification, with cards good for one year. Cardholders buy from five licensed dispensaries. The program page also links a waiver form for the limit of 4.5 grams of THC per 90 days.

Iowa Department of Health and Human Services, Medical cannabis patients and caregivers · checked November 15, 2026 [2] Official source

Can a past cannabis record be cleared?

Labels name the process the cited state law provides. Whether one record qualifies depends on that record.

By application Misdemeanor expunged on application after 8 years, once

Iowa courts must expunge the record of a misdemeanor case on the defendant’s application, as a matter of law, once more than eight years have passed since the conviction, if no charges are pending, the person has not had two deferred judgments, and all fines, fees and restitution are paid (Iowa Code § 901C.3). It may be used once in a lifetime, though one application can cover misdemeanors from the same incident; the list of excluded offenses (such as some alcohol, driving, sex, assault and weapons offenses) does not include drug offenses. Charges that ended in acquittal or dismissal can be expunged on application, or on the court’s own motion, 180 days later once costs are paid (§ 901C.2). Expunged records are confidential, and the conviction is removed from the Department of Public Safety’s criminal history files. The law covers criminal records in general and does not name marijuana.

Iowa Code §§ 901C.2 and 901C.3 · checked November 16, 2026 [3] Official source
