Cannabis laws in the Netherlands

Planning a trip to the Netherlands, or live there? Adults: Tolerated, not legal · Buying: Coffeeshops, 5 grams per person per day · Carrying: Up to 5 grams is not prosecuted. The coffeeshop list, town by town, is further down.

Cannabis is still illegal under the Opium Act, but since the 1970s the state has tolerated small-scale use and sales in licensed coffeeshops. Growing for those shops was never allowed; a state experiment with licensed growers started in 2025.

Dutch sources are summarized in English.

What is different about the Netherlands

  • Nothing about cannabis is actually legal. The famous Dutch model is a written promise by prosecutors not to act, under rules known as AHOJG-I: shops may not advertise, sell hard drugs, cause nuisance, admit minors or sell large quantities, and must serve residents only.
  • The back door problem: coffeeshops may sell, but growing is illegal, so for decades their supply came from the illegal market. The state experiment is the first attempt to fix that.
  • Tourists can be turned away. Coffeeshops are meant for residents of the Netherlands, though towns decide how strictly to enforce it.
  • Medical cannabis has been sold in ordinary pharmacies since 2003, earlier than almost anywhere else.
  • Driving uses a fixed blood limit for THC, with zero tolerance for mixing drugs or drugs and alcohol.

The law in the Netherlands

Can adults use it without a prescription?

Limited Tolerated, not legal

Possessing, selling and growing cannabis are all offences. Prosecutors do not pursue adults for small personal amounts, and coffeeshops that follow the national rules are left alone. [1][8]

Government of the Netherlands; Rijksoverheid · checked November 19, 2026

Is there a medical program?

Allowed Yes, by prescription since 2003

Since September 1, 2003, pharmacies have been able to dispense medicinal cannabis to patients with a doctor's prescription. The government's Office of Medicinal Cannabis supplies standardized, pharmaceutical-grade cannabis, and every Dutch pharmacy can dispense it. [6][9]

Cannabis and Cannabinoid Research (2018); Al Jazeera, Sep 1 2003 (news) · checked November 19, 2026

Where can adults buy it legally?

Limited Coffeeshops, 5 grams per person per day

A coffeeshop may sell no more than 5 grams per person per day, keep no more than 500 grams in stock and admit only residents of the Netherlands. Whether the residents-only rule is enforced differs from town to town, and each town decides whether to allow coffeeshops at all. [10][2]

Business.gov.nl; Government of the Netherlands · checked November 19, 2026

How much can an adult carry?

Limited Up to 5 grams is not prosecuted

The Public Prosecution Service does not prosecute people for possessing no more than 5 grams of cannabis. [2][8]

Government of the Netherlands; Rijksoverheid · checked November 19, 2026

Can adults grow it at home?

Limited Illegal, but 5 plants are not prosecuted

The government says growing cannabis at home or anywhere else is not permitted. Its toleration policy still says prosecutors do not pursue people for no more than five plants. Police can seize the plants. [1][2][11]

Government of the Netherlands; DutchNews.nl, 2011 Supreme Court ruling (news) · checked November 19, 2026

What are the driving rules?

Not permitted 3 micrograms of THC per litre of blood; zero if combined

Driving under the influence of drugs is prohibited. Since July 1, 2017 there have been fixed blood limits for the most common drugs; for THC the limit is 3 µg per litre of blood. Using several drugs, or drugs with alcohol, triggers a zero limit. Police screen with a saliva test and confirm with a blood test. Two convictions in five years cancel your licence. [7][12][13]

Government of the Netherlands; Netherlands Forensic Institute; CBR · checked November 19, 2026

Coffeeshops: where they are

At the end of 2024 the Netherlands had 563 tolerated coffeeshops in 103 of its 342 municipalities. Most towns allow none. Amsterdam alone had 167, about 3 in every 10. [14]

Names are as the city records them, which is sometimes the company name rather than the sign over the door. Sorted by postcode, so shops near each other sit together. [15]

    Good to know

    The Controlled Cannabis Supply Chain Experiment entered its experimental phase on April 7, 2025, which fixes its end date at April 7, 2029. Coffeeshops in the participating towns may now buy, sell and stock cannabis only from designated growers, and the 500 gram stock limit does not apply to them. Before the experiment ends, the government will decide the future of coffeeshop policy based on the research. [17][18][5]
