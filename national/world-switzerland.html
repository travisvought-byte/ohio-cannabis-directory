Cannabis laws in Switzerland

Planning a trip to Switzerland, or live there? Adults: Illegal, but small amounts are tolerated · Buying: Pilot trials, or low-THC hemp · Carrying: Up to 10 grams is not punished · At the border: Swiss-legal hemp can be illegal next door. Where to buy (the pilot trials) is just below.

Illegal above 1% THC, but up to 10 grams for personal use is not punished and using it is a 100 franc fine. About 10,000 adults buy legally through official city pilot trials, and a law for regulated access is still in committee.

German sources are summarized in English.

What is different about Switzerland

  • The line is 1% THC, higher than in neighbouring countries. Below it, hemp flowers are sold and bought legally.
  • Using it is fined, having it is not. An adult caught using can get a 100 franc penalty, while up to 10 grams for personal use is not punished.
  • Legal cannabis exists only inside research. About 10,400 adults were buying it through seven pilot trials in mid-2025.
  • Cantons differ. The federal health office says the fixed penalty has only partly made enforcement uniform.
  • Zero tolerance at the wheel, and legal hemp can still leave THC in the blood.
  • A legalization bill exists, but the committee sent it back for revision in May 2026.
  • Medical cannabis needs no special permit since 2022, but patients usually pay for it themselves.

[1][2][3][4][5][6]

Travelling? Never carry cannabis across an international border, even between two places where it is legal. See where it can land you in prison.

Where to buy: the pilot trials

Buying is legal only inside an approved scientific trial, and only for people enrolled in it. Each trial recruits its own participants in its own towns. The Federal Office of Public Health lists every approved trial; to take part, go to the trial's own site. [7]

The law in Switzerland

Can adults use it without a prescription?

Limited Illegal, but small amounts are tolerated

Cannabis with 1% THC or more is prohibited outside the official pilot trials. An adult caught using it can get a fixed penalty of 100 francs, while having up to 10 grams for personal use is not a criminal offence. Enforcement still differs from canton to canton. About 10,400 adults were in the seven ongoing trials as of June 2025. [1][2][3][8]

FOPH; ch.ch; FOPH pilot trial analysis; Chambers 2026 · checked November 19, 2026

Is there a medical program?

Allowed Yes, on a doctor's prescription since August 2022

The ban on cannabis for medical purposes was lifted on August 1, 2022. Doctors can prescribe cannabis medicines without a special permit from the health office, and pharmacies make up preparations on prescription. Patients usually pay themselves, because compulsory health insurance covers the cost only in exceptional cases. Doctors must report treatment data for the first two years, a requirement that runs until 2029. [6][2]

FOPH, Medizinische Anwendung von Cannabis; ch.ch · checked November 19, 2026

Where can adults buy it legally?

Limited Pilot trials, or low-THC hemp

The trials sell regulated cannabis to registered participants. The Bern, Biel and Lucerne pharmacy trial has been extended to October 2028. Outside the trials, only products under 1% THC, such as high-CBD flowers for smoking, can be sold and bought legally. [3][9][1]

FOPH; Swissinfo (news) · checked November 19, 2026

How much can an adult carry?

Limited Up to 10 grams is not punished

Having up to 10 grams for your own use is not a criminal offence and is not fined. Using it can still bring the 100 franc fixed penalty. Minors come under juvenile criminal law. [1][2]

FOPH; ch.ch · checked November 19, 2026

Can adults share it?

Limited Passing up to 10 grams to an adult is not fined

Supplying up to 10 grams to another adult, such as sharing a joint, is not fined. Selling it is not allowed. [2]

ch.ch · checked November 19, 2026

Can adults grow it at home?

Limited Only hemp under 1% THC

Growing your own is allowed only if the plant stays below 1% THC. Cannabis at or above that level is prohibited outside the pilot trials. [2][1]

ch.ch; FOPH · checked November 19, 2026

What are the driving rules?

Not permitted Zero tolerance: 1.5 micrograms of THC per litre of blood

Driving with THC in your blood is not allowed. Switzerland treats proven THC as unfit to drive whatever the concentration, using an analytical limit of 1.5 micrograms per litre of whole blood with a 30 percent margin for measurement uncertainty. Legal low-THC hemp can still leave measurable THC. People taking prescribed THC products get an individual fitness assessment instead. [2][4]

ch.ch; Swiss Society of Legal Medicine report for the Federal Roads Office (2023) · checked November 19, 2026

Can you take it across the border?

Limited Swiss-legal hemp can be illegal next door

Other countries, including Switzerland's neighbours, have stricter laws and lower THC limits, so products that are legal in Switzerland may be prohibited abroad. The government's advice is to check with the authorities of the country you are travelling to before taking any cannabis product with you. [2]

ch.ch · checked November 19, 2026

Good to know

A National Council committee drafted a Cannabis Products Act that would give adults legal, strictly regulated access, and put it out for consultation from August 29 to December 1, 2025. On May 8, 2026 the committee voted 16 to 8, with one abstention, to keep working on it and sent the draft back to its subcommittee for revision; it reported that a majority of cantons reject the draft in its current form. It is not law. [10][5][11]
