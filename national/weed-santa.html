Weed Santa: free cannabis help for Ohio adults who can't afford it

Can’t afford cannabis you need, or want to help someone who can’t? Weed Santa connects Ohio adults 21 and over with vetted gifters who give a legal amount for free, at a private home, under Ohio’s gifting law.

Weed Santa is Travis Vought, who runs this site. Every gifter is vetted by him. Requests start in Ohio. Nothing is sold, nothing is shipped, and no donation is ever asked for or taken in return.

How it works, in four steps

  1. You ask, privately

    Fill in the short form below. It goes straight to Weed Santa's inbox. Nothing is posted on this site.

  2. We match you in your county

    Weed Santa pairs you with a vetted gifter near you, as gifters are available. First names only until you both agree to meet.

  3. You meet at a private home

    Yours or the gifter's, or a farm. Never a parking lot, park, bar, hall, hotel or event.

  4. It is a gift. Full stop.

    You never pay, tip, trade or chip in for gas. Bring your ID; the gifter will check it.

Ohio's gifting rules, in plain words

Ohio lets one adult give cannabis to another only under all of these conditions. Weed Santa follows every one of them.

Both 21 or older

The person giving and the person receiving must both be at least 21. Giving to anyone younger is against the law. [1][2]

Free means free

The gift must be without remuneration: no money, no gas money, no trade, no tip, no favor in return. [1]

Up to 2.5 oz or 15 g a day

No more than 2.5 ounces of flower or 15 grams of extract to the same person on the same day. [1]

Private homes and farms only

The handoff must happen on privately owned property used mainly as a home or a farm. Every other kind of handoff needs a state license. [1]

Ohio only

Ohio's law covers a handoff on private property in Ohio. It does not cover mailing it or carrying it into another state. [1]

Share a harvest, don't grow for someone

Home growers may give from their own harvest, but Ohio bars growing or holding plants on someone else's behalf, and selling or profiting from a home grow. [3]

Not in public

Consuming it in a public place is against the law. [3]

What Weed Santa never does

  • Weed Santa never sells cannabis, never takes payment or donations for a gift, and never ships it.
  • Weed Santa never holds, stores or hands out cannabis itself. Gifts go from one Ohio adult to another.
  • Weed Santa never publishes who asked. Requests are deleted 90 days after they close.
  • Nothing sold here, ever, comes with a gift. No buy-this-get-that.

Cut the cost first

If cost is what's stopping you, these can help on their own or alongside a gift.

The state card costs nothing

Ohio dropped its medical marijuana patient and caregiver registration fee on May 15, 2024. The cost left is the visit with a certified physician. [4][5]

Say if you're a veteran or on SSI or SSDI

Indigent status (for example SSI or SSDI) and veteran status are recorded when you register. Ask your physician to mark it. [6]

Ask the dispensary about pricing

Ask about veteran, medical patient or low-income pricing before you buy. Each store sets its own terms, so ask at the counter or check the store's own page.

Become a gifter

Every gifter is vouched for by someone Weed Santa already trusts, shows ID on a video call with Weed Santa, and agrees to the gifter's code. Gifters set how often they can give. Gifters give from their own Ohio purchases or their own Ohio harvest; Weed Santa never collects or hands out cannabis.

The gifter's code

  1. Free means free

    Never take money, gas money, a trade, a tip or a favor, from the person or from anyone on their behalf.

  2. Private homes and farms only

    Never hand off in a parking lot, park, bar, hall, hotel or at an event.

  3. Check ID, every time

    Both of you 21 or older. No ID, no gift.

  4. Stay within the limit

    No more than 2.5 oz of flower or 15 g of extract to the same person in a day.

  5. Ohio only

    Give only what you bought at an Ohio dispensary or grew yourself in Ohio. Never mail it or take it across a state line.

  6. Share, don't grow for someone

    Give from your own harvest; don't grow for a particular person.

  7. Keep it private

    First names only. Never share a requester's details with anyone.

  8. Either side can say no

    Anyone can cancel at any time. Tell Weed Santa if anything feels off.

Outside Ohio

Weed Santa starts in Ohio. Other states set their own gifting limits, and Michigan and Massachusetts also bar gifts that are advertised or promoted to the public, so each state is opened only after its own law is checked. Want it in your state? Email Weed Santa and name your state.
