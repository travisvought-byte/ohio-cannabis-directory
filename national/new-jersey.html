Using it

Can you use it in public?

Limited Where cigarette smoking is allowed

Cannabis smoking is allowed where cigarette smoking is permitted unless stated otherwise, and landlords may prohibit use. Licensed consumption areas admit only people 21 and older with photo ID.

Work and past records

Does state law protect workers who use cannabis off the job?

Labels sum up what the cited state law says about employers and cannabis use away from work. They are not a ruling on any one job, employer or drug test.

Protected Cannabis use alone cannot decide hiring or firing

The Cannabis Regulatory Commission says general cannabis use cannot be a determining factor in hiring or firing someone, and that an employer may not take adverse action solely because a drug test finds cannabinoid metabolites from lawful use. Employers may keep a drug- and alcohol-free workplace and may require a drug test on reasonable suspicion of use at work, on signs of impairment, in a random testing program or after a workplace accident. A positive test together with documented signs of impairment during work hours may support action. Employers bound by a federal contract may follow federal rules.

N.J.S.A. 24:6I-52, per the Cannabis Regulatory Commission’s workplace page and guidance (September 9, 2022) · checked November 15, 2026 [4][5] Official source

Can a past cannabis record be cleared?

Labels name the process the cited state law provides. Whether one record qualifies depends on that record.

Automatic Certain marijuana and hashish cases

The New Jersey Judiciary says it expunges eligible marijuana and hashish cases automatically, with no request needed, under the Marijuana Decriminalization Law; by February 2022 it had cleared more than 362,000 cases. An expunged case leaves the public court record and need not be reported on applications for jobs, housing or college. A person who believes a case should have qualified can file a motion for judicial review, and can get a certification of expungement from the court that heard the case or the Superior Court Clerk’s Office in Trenton.

New Jersey Judiciary, Marijuana Record Expungement (February 1, 2022) · checked November 15, 2026 [6] Official source
