Is weed legal in Oklahoma?

Adult use: Medical patients only No
Medical use: Allowed Yes

Official state site: Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority (Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority, checked November 15, 2026)

How patients get a medical card in Oklahoma

From Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority. Adults without a card cannot buy here.

Steps

  1. Have a physician complete the patient recommendation form.
  2. Apply online with OMMA, with proof of identity, proof of Oklahoma residency and a photo.
  3. Buy from any licensed dispensary with your patient license.

Steps as the state describes them [1]

Who can get one
Oklahoma residents with a physician recommendation form signed within 30 days of applying. [2]
Cost
$100 (plus a $4.30 card-processing fee). $20 for patients on Medicaid/SoonerSelect or Medicare and for 100% disabled veterans. [2]
Help with the cost
$20 instead of $100 with proof of Medicaid (SoonerSelect) or Medicare enrollment, or 100% disabled veteran status. [2]
How long it lasts
Two years, or 60 days for a short-term license. [3]
Caregivers
Allowed, with no application fee. A patient can have one caregiver at a time, and the physician must certify the need for one. [2]
Visiting patients
Patients with a state-issued card from another state can get a 30-day temporary Oklahoma license ($100) to buy, use and grow. [3]
Where patients buy
Licensed dispensaries. Show your patient license every time you buy. [1]

The law in Oklahoma

The same 10 questions are asked of every state. Each answer names its source and the date it was checked.

Is it legal here?

Can adults 21 and older use cannabis without a medical card?

Medical patients only No

Oklahoma has no adult-use law. In March 2023, more than 61% of voters rejected State Question 820, which would have legalized recreational marijuana. National compilations list no adult use as of November 2026.

Oklahoma State Senate (2023); Ballotpedia; DISA national map · checked November 15, 2026 [4][5][6] Official source and national compilation

Is there a state medical cannabis program?

Allowed Yes

The Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority regulates the state’s medical marijuana program.

Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority · checked November 15, 2026 [7] Official source

How much can an adult have?

Medical patients only Patients: 3 oz on you, 8 oz at home

A licensed patient may have up to 3 ounces of marijuana on their person, 8 ounces at their residence, 1 ounce of concentrate, 72 ounces of edibles and 72 ounces of topicals.

Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority, Patient Rights & Responsibilities (63 O.S. § 420; OAC 442:10-2-8) · checked November 17, 2026 [8] Official source

Can adults share or gift it?

Not permitted No

Licensed patients are prohibited from sharing legally purchased medical marijuana.

Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority, Patient Rights & Responsibilities (OAC 442:10-2-9(a)(b)) · checked November 17, 2026 [8] Official source

Growing at home

Can adults grow it at home?

Medical patients only Patients: 6 mature plants and 6 seedlings

A licensed patient may have 6 mature marijuana plants and the harvested marijuana, plus 6 seedlings. Plants must be on property the patient owns or has the owner’s written permission to use, and must not be visible from an adjacent street.

Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority, Patient Rights & Responsibilities (63 O.S. § 427.12; OAC 442:10-2-9) · checked November 17, 2026 [8] Official source

Using it

Can you use it in public?

Limited Smoking and vaping follow tobacco rules

Smoking and vaping medical marijuana outdoors in public, or indoors at a workplace, is subject to the same state laws as smoking tobacco.

Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority, Patient Rights & Responsibilities (63 O.S. § 427.8(L); OAC 442:10-2-11) · checked November 17, 2026 [8] Official source

Work and past records

Does state law protect workers who use cannabis off the job?

Labels sum up what the cited state law says about employers and cannabis use away from work. They are not a ruling on any one job, employer or drug test.

Partly protected Medical patients only

Oklahoma’s Medical Marijuana Authority says that, unless federal law or federal funding requires otherwise, employers cannot discipline, fire or refuse to hire someone solely for holding an Oklahoma medical marijuana patient license, or solely for a positive marijuana test, unless the job has safety-sensitive duties or the patient possesses, uses or is under the influence of medical marijuana at work. Employers need not allow possession or use at work.

63 O.S. § 427.8(H) and (I), per the Oklahoma Medical Marijuana Authority’s Patient Rights & Responsibilities page · checked November 15, 2026 [9] Official source

Can a past cannabis record be cleared?

Labels name the process the cited state law provides. Whether one record qualifies depends on that record.

By application Request portal since Nov. 2026, or court petition

The Oklahoma State Bureau of Investigation describes Clean Slate as a state-initiated process that expunges certain eligible records without a court petition (22 O.S. §§ 18b and 19d). On November 1, 2026 it opened an online portal where a person asks it to expunge an eligible record; the record must show a final disposition, such as a conviction or dismissal. The Bureau’s pages do not say when records will be sealed without a request, and the statute sections could not be read from an official site, so this answer describes the request process. Court petitions under 22 O.S. §§ 18 and 19 remain; the Bureau lists no fee to expunge a court record and charges $150 to process an order expunging an arrest record. The law covers criminal records in general; neither page names cannabis offenses.

Oklahoma State Bureau of Investigation, Clean Slate Initiative (November 5, 2026) and Criminal History Record Expungement (July 23, 2026) · in effect November 1, 2026 · checked November 16, 2026 [10][11] Official source
