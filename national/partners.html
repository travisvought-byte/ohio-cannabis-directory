Work with The Reefer

The Reefer is a free, source-linked guide to cannabis law, medical cards and help in every state. Patient groups, veteran organizations, legal aid offices, clinics, libraries and state associations can be listed here and can link here, at no cost.

Get listedTell us who you help and where. We add organizations to the state pages they serve, with a link to your own site.
Link to usPoint your members to their state page, the medical card guides or the Medicaid and insurance page.
Correct usIf a rule, fee or address on a page you know is out of date, send the source and we fix it.

Ground rules

  • No paid placement, paid ranking or paid verification. Listing is free and cannot be bought.
  • A listing is not an endorsement, in either direction. Advocacy groups are named as advocacy groups.
  • Every published fact names its source and the date it was checked. Listings link to the organization’s own site.
  • The site is nonpartisan and treats every party and lawmaker the same way.
  • Everything here is free to reuse under CC BY 4.0.
