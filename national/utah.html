How patients get a medical card in Utah

From Utah Department of Health and Human Services, Center for Medical Cannabis. Adults without a card cannot buy here.

Steps

  1. Create a Utah ID and an account in the state’s electronic verification system (EVS).
  2. Meet in person with a medical provider, who submits your certification in the EVS.
  3. Pay the card fee when the emailed instructions arrive.
  4. Meet with a medical cannabis pharmacist before your first purchase, in person or by phone or video.

Steps as the state describes them [1]

Who can get one
Utah residents 18 or older with an approved medical condition. [1]
How long it lasts
One year, unless your provider sets a shorter time. [1]
Caregivers
Caregiver cards and minor patient/guardian cards are available. [2]
Visiting patients
Visiting patients with a home-state card for a listed condition can get a non-Utah resident card. It lasts 21 days, with up to two a year. [3]
Where patients buy
Licensed medical cannabis pharmacies or home delivery. [1]

The law in Utah

The same 10 questions are asked of every state. Each answer names its source and the date it was checked.

Is it legal here?

Can adults 21 and older use cannabis without a medical card?

Medical patients only No

Utah has no adult-use law. Possessing marijuana is a class B misdemeanor, and a third conviction within seven years is a class A misdemeanor. That is the law as reprinted in a January 2026 House bill; national compilations list no adult use as of November 2026.

Utah Code 58-37-8(2)(d), as reprinted in 2026 H.B. 253; Ballotpedia; DISA national map · checked November 15, 2026 [5][6][7] Official source and national compilation

Is there a state medical cannabis program?

Allowed Yes

Utah’s Center for Medical Cannabis works to give Utahns safe access to medical cannabis, and its site lists pharmacies that serve patients.

Utah Center for Medical Cannabis · checked November 15, 2026 [8] Official source

Buying and having it

Where can adults buy it legally?

Medical patients only Cardholders only, at Utah medical cannabis pharmacies

Patients can buy at any licensed medical cannabis pharmacy in Utah. It is illegal to buy cannabis in another state and bring it into Utah, even with a medical cannabis card. The provider sets a monthly limit; the highest is 113 grams of unprocessed flower and 20 grams of THC in all other products.

Utah Center for Medical Cannabis, Patients · checked November 17, 2026 [9] Official source

Using it

Can you use it in public?

Not permitted No, except in a medical emergency

Patients may not use medical cannabis in public, indoors or outdoors, unless it is a medical emergency. Smoking cannabis is not allowed; vaping is legal.

Utah Center for Medical Cannabis, Patients · checked November 17, 2026 [9] Official source

Work and past records

Does state law protect workers who use cannabis off the job?

Labels sum up what the cited state law says about employers and cannabis use away from work. They are not a ruling on any one job, employer or drug test.

Partly protected Public employees who are medical patients

Utah’s medical cannabis law says it does not require a private employer to accommodate medical cannabis use or limit a private employer’s policies restricting use by applicants or employees. When a state or local government employee or officer considers a person’s lawful medical cannabis possession or use, it may not be treated differently from lawful use of a prescribed controlled substance. The Marijuana Policy Project, an advocacy group, lists a further rule that a state or local government employee with a medical cannabis card may not be retaliated against for a failed drug test without evidence of impairment, with exceptions tied to federal funding, clearances and licenses; that rule was not found in the 2024 text of § 26B-4-207 as read and is not yet checked against the state’s own law.

Utah Code §§ 26B-4-207 and 26B-4-211; Marijuana Policy Project’s chart of state medical cannabis employment protections · checked November 15, 2026 [10][11] Official source and national compilation

Can a past cannabis record be cleared?

Labels name the process the cited state law provides. Whether one record qualifies depends on that record.

Automatic Clean Slate, including drug possession after 7 years

Utah’s courts say eligible cases are now expunged automatically, without a form: acquittals within 60 days, dismissals with prejudice within 180 days, and some convictions after a wait once fines, fees and restitution are paid, including class A drug possession after seven years (usually no more than two cases), class B misdemeanors after six, and class C misdemeanors and infractions after five. This Clean Slate law covers criminal records in general, not only cannabis. Other cases need a certificate of eligibility from the Bureau of Criminal Identification and a court petition, with fees; the page says a person convicted of cannabis possession who does not qualify for a certificate may still qualify by meeting the medical cannabis requirements. Expunged records are sealed, and a person may say the arrest or conviction never happened.

Utah State Courts, Expunging Adult Criminal Records · checked November 15, 2026 [12] Official source
