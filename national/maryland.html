How patients get a medical card in Maryland

From Maryland Cannabis Administration. Adults 21 and older can also buy without a card; see the answers below.

Steps

  1. Create an account at Maryland OneStop and verify your email.
  2. Complete the patient application online, with your documents ready to upload.
  3. After approval, you can order a patient ID card.

Steps as the state describes them [1]

Who can get one
Anyone living in Maryland whose provider recommends medical cannabis for a qualifying medical condition. [1]
Cost
$25 for the patient ID card, ordered after approval. [1]
Caregivers
A patient may name one caregiver aged 21 or older. Minor patients must have at least one caregiver who is a parent or guardian, and may have up to four. [1]
Where patients buy
Licensed Maryland dispensaries. [1]

Not stated on the state pages we checked: how long it lasts, visiting patients. Check with the state.

The same 10 questions are asked of every state. Each answer names its source and the date it was checked.

Is it legal here?

Can adults 21 and older use cannabis without a medical card?

Allowed Yes, 21 and older

The Maryland Cannabis Administration states that cannabis is legal for adults 21 and older.

Is there a state medical cannabis program?

Allowed Yes

The Maryland Cannabis Administration runs a medical cannabis program alongside adult use.

Maryland Cannabis Administration · checked November 15, 2026 [4] Official source

Buying and having it

Where can adults buy it legally?

Allowed Licensed dispensaries, with ID

Adults 21 and older may buy from a licensed dispensary after showing government-issued photo ID, up to the personal use amount.

Maryland Cannabis Administration, Adult-use cannabis FAQs · checked November 15, 2026 [5] Official source

How much can an adult have?

Allowed 1.5 oz flower, 12 g concentrate, 750 mg THC

Adults 21 and older may possess up to 1.5 ounces of flower, 12 grams of concentrated cannabis, or products with up to 750 milligrams of THC. This is the personal use amount.

Maryland Cannabis Administration, Adult-use cannabis FAQs · checked November 15, 2026 [5] Official source

Can adults share or gift it?

Allowed Personal use amount, without payment

Adults 21 and older may share up to the personal use amount with each other without payment. Gifts tied to another transaction, or offered with a sale of goods or services, do not count.

Maryland Department of Legislative Services, HB 441 fiscal and policy note (2024), current law · checked November 15, 2026 [6] Official source

Growing at home

Can adults grow it at home?

Allowed 2 plants per household

Adults 21 and older may grow up to two plants at home, out of public view. A household may have no more than two, however many adults live there.

Maryland Cannabis Administration, Adult-use cannabis FAQs · checked November 15, 2026 [5] Official source

Using it

Can you use it in public?

Limited No smoking in public spaces

Smoking cannabis is prohibited in all indoor and outdoor public spaces, including parks, event venues, bars, restaurants, streets and sidewalks.

Maryland Cannabis Administration, BeCannabisSmart guide (October 2025) · checked November 15, 2026 [7] Official source

What are the driving rules?

Not permitted Penalized like drunk driving

It is illegal to smoke or use cannabis while driving. Driving while impaired by cannabis carries the same penalties as driving drunk.

Maryland Cannabis Administration, BeCannabisSmart guide (October 2025) · checked November 15, 2026 [7] Official source

Work and past records

Does state law protect workers who use cannabis off the job?

Labels sum up what the cited state law says about employers and cannabis use away from work. They are not a ruling on any one job, employer or drug test.

Partly protected Fire and rescue workers who are patients

Chapter 183 of the 2026 session (SB 439) bars employers from firing, disciplining or otherwise discriminating against a public fire and rescue employee (firefighters, EMTs, cardiac rescue technicians and paramedics) for holding a valid medical cannabis certification or testing positive while holding one. Employers may still ban working while impaired or using cannabis on duty, and need not break federal law or lose a federal benefit. The Maryland Cannabis Administration says the adult-use law does not address workplace use or employer drug testing, so other workers remain subject to workplace policies.

Md. Code, State Gov’t § 20-606, as amended by 2026 Md. Laws ch. 183 (SB 439); Maryland Cannabis Administration, Adult-use cannabis FAQs · in effect October 1, 2026 · checked November 23, 2026 [8][9][5] Official source

Automatic Possession-only cases automatically; others by petition

Maryland removed cannabis cases in which possession was the only charge, decided before July 1, 2023, from the state’s criminal records repository by July 1, 2024, and keeps them off the Judiciary’s Case Search; the courts note that these records are not expunged at the court itself and may still appear on background checks (Criminal Procedure §§ 10-111, 10-112 and 10-401). A civil charge for possessing a personal-use amount is expunged three years after it is decided if nothing in the case ended in a conviction (§ 10-105.1). Other cannabis convictions are expunged by petition in the court where the case was decided: possession as soon as the sentence, including any probation or parole, is complete (§ 10-105), and possession with intent to distribute three years after (§ 10-110). Unpaid court fees and costs do not bar expungement, and the court waives those tied to the charge (§ 10-105.4). A pardoned possession conviction may be kept off Case Search from January 31, 2026.
