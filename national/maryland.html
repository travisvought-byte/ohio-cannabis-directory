The law in Maryland

The same 10 questions are asked of every state. Each answer names its source and the date it was checked.

Is it legal here?

Can adults 21 and older use cannabis without a medical card?

Allowed Yes, 21 and older

The Maryland Cannabis Administration states that cannabis is legal for adults 21 and older.

Maryland Cannabis Administration, BeCannabisSmart · checked November 15, 2026 [1] Official source

Is there a state medical cannabis program?

Allowed Yes

The Maryland Cannabis Administration runs a medical cannabis program alongside adult use.

Maryland Cannabis Administration · checked November 15, 2026 [2] Official source

Buying and having it

Where can adults buy it legally?

Allowed Licensed dispensaries, with ID

Adults 21 and older may buy from a licensed dispensary after showing government-issued photo ID, up to the personal use amount.

Maryland Cannabis Administration, Adult-use cannabis FAQs · checked November 15, 2026 [3] Official source

How much can an adult have?

Allowed 1.5 oz flower, 12 g concentrate, 750 mg THC

Adults 21 and older may possess up to 1.5 ounces of flower, 12 grams of concentrated cannabis, or products with up to 750 milligrams of THC. This is the personal use amount.

Maryland Cannabis Administration, Adult-use cannabis FAQs · checked November 15, 2026 [3] Official source

Can adults share or gift it?

Allowed Personal use amount, without payment

Adults 21 and older may share up to the personal use amount with each other without payment. Gifts tied to another transaction, or offered with a sale of goods or services, do not count.

Maryland Department of Legislative Services, HB 441 fiscal and policy note (2024), current law · checked November 15, 2026 [4] Official source

Growing at home

Can adults grow it at home?

Allowed 2 plants per household

Adults 21 and older may grow up to two plants at home, out of public view. A household may have no more than two, however many adults live there.

Maryland Cannabis Administration, Adult-use cannabis FAQs · checked November 15, 2026 [3] Official source

Using it

Can you use it in public?

Limited No smoking in public spaces

Smoking cannabis is prohibited in all indoor and outdoor public spaces, including parks, event venues, bars, restaurants, streets and sidewalks.

Maryland Cannabis Administration, BeCannabisSmart guide (October 2025) · checked November 15, 2026 [5] Official source

What are the driving rules?

Not permitted Penalized like drunk driving

It is illegal to smoke or use cannabis while driving. Driving while impaired by cannabis carries the same penalties as driving drunk.

Maryland Cannabis Administration, BeCannabisSmart guide (October 2025) · checked November 15, 2026 [5] Official source

Work and past records

Does state law protect workers who use cannabis off the job?

Labels sum up what the cited state law says about employers and cannabis use away from work. They are not a ruling on any one job, employer or drug test.

Partly protected Fire and rescue workers who are patients

The Maryland Cannabis Administration says the Cannabis Reform Act does not address cannabis use or impairment in the workplace, or employer drug screening: people remain subject to existing laws and to workplace policies, including policies that ban cannabis use. The Marijuana Policy Project, an advocacy group, lists one protection, for fire and rescue employees who are certified medical cannabis patients (Md. Code, State Gov’t § 20-606); that part is not yet checked against the state’s own law.

Maryland Cannabis Administration, Adult-use cannabis FAQs; Md. Code, State Gov’t § 20-606, per the Marijuana Policy Project’s chart · checked November 15, 2026 [3][6] Official source and national compilation

Can a past cannabis record be cleared?

Labels name the process the cited state law provides. Whether one record qualifies depends on that record.

Automatic Cases where possession was the only charge

The Maryland Cannabis Administration says the 2022 and 2023 laws set up a process to expunge cases in which possession of less than 10 grams of cannabis is the only charge, and include other expungement provisions. The Marijuana Policy Project, an advocacy group, lists that expungement as automatic and due by July 2024, says the state appears to have missed the deadline, and notes that the governor’s June 2024 pardons do not themselves expunge a record; those points are not yet checked against the state’s own law.

Maryland Cannabis Administration, Adult-use cannabis FAQs; Marijuana Policy Project’s table of legalization states’ expungement laws · checked November 15, 2026 [3][7] Official source and national compilation
