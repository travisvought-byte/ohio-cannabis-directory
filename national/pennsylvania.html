The law in Pennsylvania

The same 10 questions are asked of every state. Each answer names its source and the date it was checked.

Is it legal here?

Can adults 21 and older use cannabis without a medical card?

Medical patients only No

Pennsylvania has no adult-use law. Without a medical card, possessing up to 30 grams of marijuana or 8 grams of hashish is a misdemeanor punishable by up to 30 days in jail, a fine of up to $500, or both.

Controlled Substance, Drug, Device and Cosmetic Act (Act 64 of 1972) · in effect April 14, 1972 · checked October 28, 2026 [1] Official source

Is there a state medical cannabis program?

Allowed Yes

Medical use is lawful only for a registered patient or caregiver with a Department of Health ID card and practitioner certification. A dispensary may sell no more than a 90-day supply, and smoking medical marijuana is unlawful.

Medical Marijuana Act (Act 16 of 2016) · in effect May 17, 2016 · checked October 28, 2026 [2] Official source

Growing at home

Can adults grow it at home?

Not permitted No

Only grower/processors permitted by the Department of Health may grow medical marijuana. Pennsylvania has no adult-use home-grow law.

What are the driving rules?

Not permitted Any amount in the blood is DUI

Driving with any amount of a Schedule I controlled substance or its metabolite in the blood is DUI, impaired or not. Marijuana is Schedule I under state law, and there is no exception for medical patients.

75 Pa.C.S. § 3802; Act 64 of 1972 · in effect May 11, 2006 · checked October 28, 2026 [3][1] Official source

Work and past records

Does state law protect workers who use cannabis off the job?

Labels sum up what the cited state law says about employers and cannabis use away from work. They are not a ruling on any one job, employer or drug test.

Partly protected Certified medical patients only

An employer may not fire, refuse to hire or otherwise discriminate against someone solely because they are certified to use medical marijuana. Employers need not allow use at work and may discipline employees for being under the influence at work when their conduct falls below the standard of care for the job.

Medical Marijuana Act (Act 16 of 2016), section 2103(b) · in effect May 17, 2016 · checked October 28, 2026 [2] Official source

Can a past cannabis record be cleared?

Labels name the process the cited state law provides. Whether one record qualifies depends on that record.

Automatic Clean Slate seals eligible records

Clean Slate limits access to (seals) certain records automatically: charges that did not end in a conviction; summary convictions after five years; and second- and third-degree misdemeanors and misdemeanors punishable by up to two years after seven years free of convictions punishable by a year or more. Court-ordered restitution must be paid. A pardon is a separate step. The law covers criminal records in general, not only cannabis cases; whether one record qualifies depends on the full record and the exclusions in the statute.

18 Pa.C.S. § 9122.2, as amended by Act 36 of 2023 · in effect June 11, 2024 · checked October 28, 2026 [4] Official source
