Cannabis laws in Germany

Planning a trip to Germany, or live there? Adults: Yes, 18 and older · Buying: No shops. Non-profit clubs only · Carrying: 25 grams in public, 50 grams at home · At the border: Importing is still banned. Where to join (licensed clubs) is just below.

Legal for adults since April 1, 2024, without shops. Adults can carry some, grow a few plants at home, or join a non-profit cultivation club.

What is different about Germany

  • Legal to have and grow, but there are no shops. The only legal sources are your own three plants, a non-profit club, or a prescription.
  • Where you light up matters. No smoking within 100 metres of the entrance to schools, playgrounds, youth centres or public sports grounds, and none in pedestrian zones between 7am and 8pm.
  • The clubs have been slow to start. Only 366 had been approved nationwide by October 31, 2025.
  • Medical cannabis became the easy route instead. Imports of medical flower rose 170 percent from the first to the second half of 2024 as online prescription platforms spread, and the government now wants to restrict them.
  • Driving has a hard number: 3.5 ng/ml of THC in blood serum, and zero for new and young drivers.
  • Old convictions for things that are now legal can be wiped from the federal register on request.

[1][2][3][4][5][6]

Travelling? Never carry cannabis across an international border, even between two places where it is legal. See where it can land you in prison.

Where to join: licensed clubs

Germany has no cannabis shops. Adults get it by joining a licensed non-profit cultivation association near them. Each state licenses its own clubs, and most do not publish a list. Lower Saxony and Hamburg do, and both lists are below. [7][8]

The law in Germany

Can adults use it without a prescription?

Allowed Yes, 18 and older

The Cannabis Act took effect on April 1, 2024. Since then, adults in Germany can legally use cannabis. [9][10]

Cannabisgesetz (CanG); Federal Government FAQ · checked November 19, 2026

Is there a medical program?

Allowed Yes, under the Medical Cannabis Act

Since April 1, 2024 cannabis is no longer listed under the narcotics law. Medical cannabis is governed by its own law, the Medizinal-Cannabisgesetz (MedCanG). [11]

Federal Ministry of Health, Cannabis als Medizin · checked November 19, 2026

Where can adults buy it legally?

Limited No shops. Non-profit clubs only

Cultivation associations have been allowed to grow since July 1, 2024 and may pass cannabis only to their members. Members get at most 25 grams a day and 50 grams a month; members aged 18 to 20 get at most 30 grams a month, with no more than 10 percent THC. [12][13]

Federal Ministry of Health FAQ; Federal Government FAQ · checked November 19, 2026

How much can an adult carry?

Allowed 25 grams in public, 50 grams at home

Adults may carry up to 25 grams in public and keep up to 50 grams at home. Going a little over (up to 30 grams out, up to 60 grams at home) is a fine. Beyond that it is a crime with up to three years in prison. [9][14]

Federal Government FAQ; Lower Saxony state government · checked November 19, 2026

Can adults grow it at home?

Allowed 3 plants per adult

Adults may grow up to three plants at home for their own use. The plants and harvest must be protected from children, teenagers and other people. [2][9]

Federal Ministry of Health, Feb 23 2024; Federal Government FAQ · checked November 19, 2026

What are the driving rules?

Not permitted 3.5 ng/ml THC in blood serum; zero for new drivers

Since August 22, 2024 it is an offence to drive with 3.5 ng/ml of THC or more in blood serum. The standard penalty is a 500 euro fine and a one-month driving ban; mixed with alcohol, the standard fine is 1,000 euros. Drivers on probation and anyone under 21 face a total ban, with a standard 250 euro fine. Patients taking prescribed cannabis are exempt. [5][15][16]

StVG § 24a (BGBl. 2024 I Nr. 266); Legal Tribune Online; Deutsches Ärzteblatt · checked November 19, 2026

Can an old cannabis record be cleared?

Allowed Old convictions can be erased on request

Since January 1, 2025, a person convicted under the old narcotics law for something that is no longer a crime can ask the public prosecutor to confirm that the entry can be erased from the federal criminal register. The prosecutor then tells the register to delete it. Lawyers say it is unclear which entries qualify. [17][6][18][19]

KCanG §§ 40 and 41; CanG Art. 15; Legal Tribune Online (news) · checked November 19, 2026

Can you take it across the border?

Not permitted Importing is still banned

Bringing cannabis into Germany from abroad remains prohibited. [13]

Federal Government FAQ · checked November 19, 2026

Good to know

A federal evaluation called EKOCAN is running until 2028. Its second interim report, on April 1, 2026, found that cultivation clubs still play only a minor role in pushing back the illegal market; the health minister called the law a dud. A separate bill to stop telemedicine prescriptions and mail-order delivery of medical cannabis flower was introduced in December 2025 and was still in Parliament at our last check. [20][21][4][22]
