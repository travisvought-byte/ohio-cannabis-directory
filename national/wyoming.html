Is it legal here?

Can adults 21 and older use cannabis without a medical card?

Not permitted No

Wyoming has no adult-use law. Possessing up to 3 ounces of marijuana in plant form is a misdemeanor punishable by up to 12 months in jail, a $1,000 fine, or both. Larger amounts are a felony punishable by up to 5 years in prison, a $10,000 fine, or both.

W.S. 35-7-1031(c) · checked November 15, 2026 [1] Official source

Is there a state medical cannabis program?

Not permitted No medical marijuana law

Wyoming law bars practitioners from prescribing or dispensing marijuana or THC except FDA-approved drugs such as dronabinol. Its former article on supervised medical use of hemp extracts (W.S. 35-7-1901 to 35-7-1903) was repealed in 2019, and hemp products are now outside the controlled substances law.

W.S. 35-7-1031(c) and 35-7-1063 · checked November 15, 2026 [1] Official source

Can a past cannabis record be cleared?

Labels name the process the cited state law provides. Whether one record qualifies depends on that record.

By application Court petition after 1, 5 or 10 years; once per statute

Wyoming does not expunge records automatically; a petition must be filed with the court, served and granted by a judge. A misdemeanor conviction can be expunged at least five years after the sentence ends (one year for a status offense) if no gun was involved and the person is not a substantial danger (W.S. 7-13-1501). An eligible felony takes at least ten years after the sentence, any court-ordered program and restitution are complete, and a list of felonies is excluded (W.S. 7-13-1502). A person can get only one expungement under each statute. The courts’ page does not state a filing fee and does not mention marijuana; the law covers criminal records in general.

Wyoming Judicial Branch, Expungements (W.S. 7-13-1501 and 7-13-1502) · checked November 16, 2026 [2] Official source
