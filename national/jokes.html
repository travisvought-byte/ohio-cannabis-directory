Joke of the day

One cannabis joke a day. Today’s is on the home page; the ones that already ran are below, newest first.

    Why does the Reefer’s flag fly so high? Look who’s holding the rope.
  • December 15, 2026
    Our ship’s crew passed the inspection. The inspector, however, is still coughing.
  • December 14, 2026
    What do you call a relaxed pirate? High seas certified.
  • December 13, 2026
    I asked my pipe for advice. It said, “Clean up your act.” Rude, but fair.
  • December 12, 2026
    Why are growers so good at keeping secrets? They know how to keep things under the canopy.
  • December 11, 2026
    I said I’d only have one cookie. In my defense, I didn’t say one at a time.
  • December 10, 2026
    What’s a stoner’s favorite kind of math? Rolling averages.
  • December 9, 2026
    My rosin press and I agree on one thing: good things come to those who apply pressure gently.
  • December 8, 2026
    Why did the pothead cross the road? He didn’t. He’s still thinking about the chicken.
  • December 7, 2026
    I named my plant Wi-Fi. The closer I get to harvest, the stronger the connection.
  • December 6, 2026
    A joint walks into a bar. The bartender says, “You look a little burnt out.”
  • December 5, 2026
    What’s the difference between a chef and a stoner at midnight? The chef has a plan.
  • December 4, 2026
    I bought a scale to be more precise. Now I know exactly how much I’ve lost in the couch.
  • December 3, 2026
    Why was the dispensary line so calm? Everyone in it was already working on their patience.
  • December 2, 2026
    My dog doesn’t smoke, but he does stare at the wall with me. That’s friendship.
  • December 1, 2026
    What did the bong say to the lighter? You really know how to get me bubbling.

    I tried a strain called “Productivity.” I cleaned one drawer for two hours and felt incredible about it.
  • November 29, 2026
    How do you know a gardener grows weed? Their tomatoes have never been so well cared for.
  • November 28, 2026
    The munchies aren’t a side effect. They’re a second hobby.
  • November 27, 2026
    Why did the brownie go to therapy? It had too many unresolved batches.
  • November 26, 2026
    I put my stash somewhere safe. If anyone finds out where, please tell me.
  • November 25, 2026
    What’s a sativa’s favorite exercise? Running through every idea it has ever had, all at once.
  • November 24, 2026
    I started a band called Indica. We’re mostly known for our slow songs and never finishing a set.
  • November 23, 2026
    Why don’t rolling papers ever win arguments? They always fold under pressure.
  • November 22, 2026
    My grinder and I have a lot in common. We both fall apart when someone turns us the wrong way.
  • November 21, 2026
    I asked the budtender for something to help me focus. He’s still talking and I’ve forgotten the question.
