Cannabis laws in Spain

Not legal, but private use and private growing are not crimes. Hundreds of private cannabis clubs operate in a legal gray zone that courts and police are tightening.

Spanish sources are summarized in English.

What is different about Spain

  • Two tracks and no legal market. In public it is an administrative offence with a fine, supply is a crime, and private use sits in between, unpunished.
  • The fines are steep. A public-use ticket starts at €601 and can reach €30,000, though paying within 15 days halves it.
  • No number is safe. There is no legal carry limit; courts work from guideline amounts and the facts of each case.
  • A plant on the balcony can cost you. Growing where the public can see it is fined on the same scale as public use.
  • The clubs are tolerated, not licensed. Their status depends on the case, the judge and the region, and they may not advertise or recruit tourists.
  • Medical cannabis arrived late and narrow: hospital specialists only, from October 2025.
  • Drivers are punished for presence alone: €1,000 and 6 points, with prison possible when the driving was under the influence.

[1][2][3][4][5][6][7][8][9]

Travelling? Never carry cannabis across an international border, even between two places where it is legal. See where it can land you in prison.

Can adults use it without a prescription?

Limited Private use is not a crime

Spain has not legalized cannabis. Using or having drugs in public places, streets, public premises or public transport is a serious administrative offence even when it is not for trafficking, and the Criminal Code punishes growing, making and trafficking. Using and growing for yourself in private is not criminal. [1][2][3]

Ley Orgánica 4/2015 art. 36.16; Código Penal art. 368; Wikipedia, Cannabis in Spain (compilation) · checked November 19, 2026

Is there a medical program?

Limited Hospital-only cannabis preparations since October 2025

Royal Decree 903/2025, in force since October 10, 2025, allows standardised cannabis preparations as pharmacy-compounded formulas. Only hospital specialist doctors may prescribe them, for the indications in the medicines agency's monographs and when authorised medicines do not work well enough, and only hospital pharmacy services prepare and dispense them. The decree covers standardised extracts, with cannabis flower only as the starting material. The agency, AEMPS, keeps a public register of the preparations. [8]

Real Decreto 903/2025 arts. 2, 4, 6, 7, 8 and 9 · checked November 19, 2026

Where can adults buy it legally?

Not permitted Selling is a crime

There are no legal shops. Trafficking in drugs, or otherwise promoting or facilitating their illegal use, is punished with one to three years in prison and a fine of up to twice the drug's value where the substance is not one that causes serious harm to health, and three to six years where it is. Courts may go one grade lower for minor cases. Import of any amount is also a crime. [2][3]

Código Penal art. 368; Wikipedia, Cannabis in Spain (compilation) · checked November 19, 2026

How much can an adult carry?

Limited No legal amount in public; fines from €601

Having or using cannabis in a public place brings a fine of €601 to €30,000 and confiscation, even with no intent to sell. Paying within 15 days cuts the fine by half, and a minor's fine can be suspended for treatment or re-education. There is no fixed legal amount. Courts use guideline quantities to separate personal use from supply, about 25 grams for hashish, and judge each case on its facts. [1][4]

Ley Orgánica 4/2015 arts. 36.16, 39 and 54 and disposición adicional quinta; El Derecho on a 2023 Murcia ruling (news) · checked November 19, 2026

Can adults grow it at home?

Limited Not a crime in private for yourself; fined if visible

Growing in a place visible to the public is a serious administrative offence, fined €601 to €30,000, unless it amounts to a crime. Growing to supply others is a crime under Article 368. A Murcia court in 2023 upheld one- and two-year prison sentences for a grow of more than 100 plants, calling the number a strong sign of trafficking. [1][2][4][3]

Ley Orgánica 4/2015 arts. 36.18 and 39; Código Penal art. 368; El Derecho (news); Wikipedia (compilation) · checked November 19, 2026

What are the driving rules?

Not permitted Any presence of drugs: €1,000 and 6 points

Driving with drugs present in the body is an administrative offence: a €1,000 fine and 6 licence points. Driving under the influence of drugs is a crime, with three to six months in prison, or a fine, or 31 to 90 days of community service, plus a driving ban of more than one and up to four years. Refusing the test is also a crime: six months to a year in prison and the same ban. [9][2]

DGT; Código Penal arts. 379.2 and 383 · checked November 19, 2026

Can an old cannabis record be cleared?

Limited General rule: records are cancelled after a crime-free period

We found no cannabis-specific scheme. Under the general rule, a person who has completed their sentence has the right to have the criminal record cancelled by the Ministry of Justice after a period without reoffending: six months for light penalties, two years for penalties of up to twelve months, three years for other less serious penalties under three years, five years for less serious penalties of three years or more, and ten years for serious ones. It can be done on request or by the ministry on its own. [2]

Código Penal art. 136 · checked November 19, 2026

Good to know

Spain's cannabis social clubs are private, members-only associations that rely on the legality of private use and shared cultivation. Their status depends on the case, the judge and the region, and a 2026 study describes their decline after years of growth. Advertising and recruiting tourists are not allowed. [5][6][7]
