How patients get a medical card in Illinois

From Illinois Department of Public Health, Medical Cannabis Patient Program. Adults 21 and older can also buy without a card; see the answers below.

Veterans who get their care at a VA facility do not need a physician’s written certification. They submit their VA medical records for the last 12 months instead. [1]

Who can get one
Illinois residents who meet the program requirements. The Department reviews applications and issues registry cards, which patients print or download from their online account. [2]
Help with the cost
Veterans with an honorable or general discharge who are eligible for VA services get a reduced application fee, with a copy of their DD-214. [1]

Not stated on the state pages we checked: cost, how long it lasts, caregivers, visiting patients, where patients buy. Check with the state.

The law in Illinois

The same 10 questions are asked of every state. Each answer names its source and the date it was checked.

Is it legal here?

Can adults 21 and older use cannabis without a medical card?

Allowed Yes, 21 and older

Adults 21 and older may buy cannabis. Residents and non-residents have different possession limits.

Illinois Cannabis Regulation Oversight Officer, FAQs · checked November 15, 2026 [4] Official source

Is there a state medical cannabis program?

Allowed Yes

Illinois registers medical cannabis patients and caregivers. 191 dispensaries were licensed to sell medical cannabis as of November 1, 2026.

Illinois Cannabis Regulation Oversight Officer · checked November 15, 2026 [5] Official source

Buying and having it

Where can adults buy it legally?

Allowed Licensed dispensaries

291 dispensaries were licensed to sell cannabis to adults 21 and older as of November 1, 2026.

Illinois Cannabis Regulation Oversight Officer · checked November 15, 2026 [5] Official source

How much can an adult have?

Allowed Residents 60 g flower; visitors 30 g

Residents over 21: 60 grams of flower, 1,000 milligrams of THC in infused products and 10 grams of concentrate. Non-residents: 30 grams, 500 milligrams and 5 grams. The amounts are cumulative.

Illinois Cannabis Regulation Oversight Officer, FAQs · checked November 15, 2026 [4] Official source

Can adults share or gift it?

Allowed Gifts allowed, non-commercial only

Adults may give cannabis as a purely non-commercial gift to another adult 21 or older. Medical cannabis patients may not gift their medical cannabis.

Illinois Cannabis Regulation Oversight Officer, FAQs · checked November 15, 2026 [4] Official source

Growing at home

Can adults grow it at home?

Medical patients only Registered patients only, up to 5 plants

Only registered qualifying medical cannabis patients may grow, up to five plants at home.

Illinois Cannabis Regulation Oversight Officer, FAQs · checked November 15, 2026 [4] Official source

Using it

Can you use it in public?

Not permitted No

The law does not permit using cannabis in any public place, and cannabis cannot be used in a motor vehicle.

Illinois Cannabis Regulation Oversight Officer, FAQs · checked November 15, 2026 [4] Official source

What are the driving rules?

Not permitted Illegal at 5 ng/ml THC in blood

Driving is prohibited with 5 nanograms of THC per milliliter of whole blood, or 10 nanograms in another bodily substance, within two hours of driving.

Illinois Cannabis Regulation Oversight Officer, FAQs · checked November 15, 2026 [4] Official source

Work and past records

Does state law protect workers who use cannabis off the job?

Labels sum up what the cited state law says about employers and cannabis use away from work. They are not a ruling on any one job, employer or drug test.

Partly protected Off-duty use, subject to workplace drug policies

The Illinois Department of Labor says the Right to Privacy in the Workplace Act bars employers from refusing to hire, firing or otherwise disadvantaging a person for using lawful products off the job site during non-work and non-call hours, except as other law provides, including section 10-50 of the Cannabis Regulation and Tax Act, which was not reviewed. The Marijuana Policy Project, an advocacy group, lists Illinois as also barring employers from penalizing someone solely for being a registered medical patient or caregiver, while letting employers enforce drug-testing, zero-tolerance and drug-free workplace policies applied without discrimination, with an exception for federal law or loss of a federal benefit; those points are not yet checked against the state’s own law.

820 ILCS 55/5, per the Illinois Department of Labor’s Right to Privacy in the Workplace Act page; Marijuana Policy Project’s chart of state medical cannabis employment protections · checked November 15, 2026 [6][7] Official source and national compilation

Can a past cannabis record be cleared?

Labels name the process the cited state law provides. Whether one record qualifies depends on that record.

Automatic Under 30 grams: automatic if no conviction

Illinois’s cannabis site says minor cannabis offenses that did not end in a conviction, usually possession, manufacture, delivery or intent to deliver under 30 grams, are expunged automatically and removed from the public record. Minor offenses that ended in a conviction may be pardoned by the Governor, which expunges the record, and county State’s Attorneys may ask courts to vacate and expunge convictions. The site names New Leaf Illinois, a statewide, state-funded initiative, for free legal help.
