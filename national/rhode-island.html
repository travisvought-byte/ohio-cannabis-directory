The law in Rhode Island

The same 10 questions are asked of every state. Each answer names its source and the date it was checked.

Is it legal here?

Can adults 21 and older use cannabis without a medical card?

Allowed Yes, 21 and older

Adult use has been legal since December 1, 2022 for people at least 21 years old.

Rhode Island Cannabis Control Commission, Frequently asked questions · in effect December 1, 2022 · checked November 15, 2026 [1] Official source

Is there a state medical cannabis program?

Allowed Yes

The Cannabis Control Commission regulates both adult-use and medical marijuana.

Rhode Island Cannabis Control Commission, Frequently asked questions · checked November 15, 2026 [1] Official source

Buying and having it

Where can adults buy it legally?

Allowed Licensed compassion centers

Licensed compassion centers are the retail stores currently selling adult-use cannabis.

Rhode Island Cannabis Control Commission, Frequently asked questions · checked November 15, 2026 [1] Official source

How much can an adult have?

Allowed 1 ounce carried, 10 at home

Adults 21 and older may carry up to 1 ounce of flower, or the equivalent in concentrate, and keep up to 10 ounces at home.

Rhode Island Department of Health, Cannabis information for adults · checked November 15, 2026 [2] Official source

Can adults share or gift it?

Allowed Up to 1 ounce, free

Adults may give away up to 1 ounce of cannabis, or the equivalent in concentrate, to another adult 21 or older without payment, as long as the transfer is not advertised or promoted.

R.I. Gen. Laws § 21-28.11-22(a)(5) · checked November 15, 2026 [3] Official source

Growing at home

Can adults grow it at home?

Allowed 3 mature and 3 immature plants

Adults 21 and older may grow up to three mature and three immature plants.

Rhode Island Department of Health, Cannabis information for adults · checked November 15, 2026 [2] Official source

Using it

Can you use it in public?

Limited No smoking or vaping in public

State law does not authorize smoking or vaping cannabis in any public place, and both are banned wherever smoking or vaping tobacco is banned.

R.I. Gen. Laws § 21-28.11-22(e) · checked November 15, 2026 [3] Official source

What are the driving rules?

Not permitted No driving under the influence

The law does not permit operating a vehicle, boat or aircraft under the influence of cannabis. Metabolites in a person’s system do not by themselves mean the person is under the influence.

R.I. Gen. Laws § 21-28.11-29 · checked November 15, 2026 [4] Official source

Work and past records

Does state law protect workers who use cannabis off the job?

Labels sum up what the cited state law says about employers and cannabis use away from work. They are not a ruling on any one job, employer or drug test.

Protected Use outside work, with exceptions

An employer may not fire or discipline an employee solely for private, lawful cannabis use outside the workplace, as long as the employee has not worked and is not working under the influence. The rule gives way to a collective bargaining agreement that prohibits such use, and to federal contractors that would lose a federal benefit. For jobs that are hazardous, dangerous or essential to public welfare and safety, an employer may bar use in the 24 hours before a shift. Employers may act on a violation of a workplace drug policy or on working under the influence.

R.I. Gen. Laws § 21-28.11-29(d) and (e) · in effect May 25, 2022 · checked November 15, 2026 [4] Official source

Can a past cannabis record be cleared?

Labels name the process the cited state law provides. Whether one record qualifies depends on that record.

Automatic Possession convictions and civil violations

The Rhode Island Judiciary says the Rhode Island Cannabis Act of 2022 requires the courts to expunge eligible cases and charges automatically, at no cost. Under the Supreme Court’s Executive Order 2023-02 that covers criminal convictions for marijuana possession by people 18 and over and civil violations by people 21 and over, and not other marijuana convictions such as selling, delivering or manufacturing. Cases where possession was the only charge were cleared before April 30, 2023; the Judiciary said cases with other charges were on track for July 1, 2024. A person can check the Judiciary’s Public Portal, where an expunged case no longer appears, and can ask the court that heard the case to act sooner by filing a Notice for Expedited Marijuana Possession Expungement.

Rhode Island Cannabis Act (2022) and Supreme Court Executive Order 2023-02, per the Rhode Island Judiciary’s release on phase one of marijuana expungement · checked November 15, 2026 [5] Official source
