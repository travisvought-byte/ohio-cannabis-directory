How patients get a medical card in Maine

From Maine Office of Cannabis Policy, Medical Use of Cannabis Program. Adults 21 and older can also buy without a card; see the answers below.

Visiting patients
Visiting patients show their home state’s registry card or other state-issued patient document, if their home state allows it. They may buy up to 2.5 ounces every 15 days. The Office lists the approved states. [1]
Where patients buy
Registered caregivers and medical dispensaries. Medical cannabis in Maine is not subject to mandatory contaminant testing or statewide inventory tracking. [2]

The law in Maine

The same 10 questions are asked of every state. Each answer names its source and the date it was checked.

Is it legal here?

Can adults 21 and older use cannabis without a medical card?

Allowed Yes, 21 and older

Maine’s adult use program serves consumers 21 and older, who may possess up to 2.5 ounces.

Maine Office of Cannabis Policy, Frequently asked questions · checked November 15, 2026 [3] Official source

Is there a state medical cannabis program?

Allowed Yes

Maine runs both a medical cannabis program and an adult use program.

Maine Office of Cannabis Policy, Frequently asked questions · checked November 15, 2026 [3] Official source

Buying and having it

Where can adults buy it legally?

Allowed Cannabis stores, up to 2.5 ounces

Adults 21 and older may buy up to 2.5 ounces from a licensed cannabis store, no more than 10 grams of it concentrate.

28-B M.R.S. § 1501(1)(F) · checked November 15, 2026 [4] Official source

How much can an adult have?

Allowed 2.5 ounces (up to 10 g concentrate)

Adults 21 and older may possess up to 2.5 ounces, of which up to 10 grams may be concentrate.

Maine Office of Cannabis Policy, Frequently asked questions · checked November 15, 2026 [3] Official source

Can adults share or gift it?

Allowed Up to 2.5 ounces, free

Adults may transfer up to 2.5 ounces, no more than 10 grams of it concentrate, to another adult 21 or older without remuneration. Remuneration includes a donation or other payment received as part of the transfer.

28-B M.R.S. § 1501(1)(C) · checked November 15, 2026 [4] Official source

Growing at home

Can adults grow it at home?

Allowed 6 mature and 12 immature plants

Each resident 21 or older may grow up to six mature plants, 12 immature plants and an unlimited number of seedlings.

Maine Office of Cannabis Policy, Frequently asked questions · checked November 15, 2026 [3] Official source

Using it

Can you use it in public?

Not permitted No

Use in any form is prohibited in public places. Neither the driver nor passengers may consume cannabis in a vehicle on a public way.

Maine Office of Cannabis Policy, Frequently asked questions · checked November 15, 2026 [3] Official source

What are the driving rules?

Not permitted OUI applies; no use in vehicles

Driving under the influence of cannabis is illegal and can bring an OUI charge. The driver and passengers may not consume cannabis in a vehicle on a public way.

Maine Office of Cannabis Policy, Frequently asked questions; 28-B M.R.S. § 1501(2)(B) · checked November 15, 2026 [3][4] Official source

Work and past records

Does state law protect workers who use cannabis off the job?

Labels sum up what the cited state law says about employers and cannabis use away from work. They are not a ruling on any one job, employer or drug test.

Partly protected Medical patients and caregivers only

Maine’s medical cannabis law says an employer may not refuse to employ or otherwise penalize a person solely for being a qualifying patient or a caregiver, unless that would put the employer in violation of federal law or cost it a federal contract or funding. The same subsection allows limits on administering or growing cannabis on premises where that would be inconsistent with their general use, and lets a business owner who bans all smoking and posts notice ban smoking medical cannabis too. What the adult-use law says about employers has not been reviewed.

22 M.R.S. § 2430-C(3) · checked November 15, 2026 [5] Official source

Can a past cannabis record be cleared?

Labels name the process the cited state law provides. Whether one record qualifies depends on that record.

By application Some pre-2017 marijuana convictions, sealing

Maine’s courts say sealing is not automatic: a person files a Motion to Seal Criminal History (form CR-218) with the clerk of the court where the conviction occurred, and the court holds a hearing. Eligible marijuana convictions are certain Class D possession and cultivation crimes, listed by statute section on the courts’ page, committed before January 30, 2017. At least four years must have passed since the sentence was fully completed, with no later convictions and no pending charges. A sealed record is not erased, since Maine has no expungement, but it is not disclosed to employers, landlords, lenders or school admissions officers, and the person may answer most inquiries as if the conviction had not occurred; criminal justice agencies and the courts can still see it.

15 M.R.S. §§ 2263 to 2265, per the State of Maine Judicial Branch’s Sealing Your Criminal Record page · checked November 15, 2026 [6] Official source
