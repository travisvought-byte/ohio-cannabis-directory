The law in Florida

The same 10 questions are asked of every state. Each answer names its source and the date it was checked.

Is it legal here?

Can adults 21 and older use cannabis without a medical card?

Medical patients only No

Florida has no adult-use law. Possessing 20 grams of cannabis or less is a first-degree misdemeanor; larger amounts are felonies.

Fla. Stat. 893.13(6) · checked November 15, 2026 [1] Official source

Is there a state medical cannabis program?

Allowed Yes

Medical marijuana and low-THC cannabis are available to qualified patients. A qualified physician orders them, and licensed medical marijuana treatment centers are the only businesses allowed to dispense them.

Florida Office of Medical Marijuana Use · checked November 15, 2026 [2] Official source

Can a past cannabis record be cleared?

Labels name the process the cited state law provides. Whether one record qualifies depends on that record.

By application Court sealing or expungement, once in a lifetime

Florida seals or expunges a criminal history record by court order. A person first applies to the Florida Department of Law Enforcement for a certificate of eligibility (a notarized application, fingerprints, certified dispositions and a $75 processing fee), then petitions the court; the Department says a record gets no relief until it receives a certified court order, and a person is entitled to one court-ordered sealing or expungement in a lifetime under ss. 943.0585 and 943.059. Separately, s. 943.0595 seals records automatically, with no limit on how many times, when no charge was filed, all counts were dismissed or dropped, or the person was acquitted of all counts, except for forcible felonies and some other offenses; the clerk sends the disposition to the Department, which seals its record. The law covers criminal records in general; none of the sources mentions marijuana.

ss. 943.0585, 943.059 and 943.0595, F.S.; Florida Department of Law Enforcement, Seal and Expunge Process · checked November 16, 2026 [3][4][5] Official source
