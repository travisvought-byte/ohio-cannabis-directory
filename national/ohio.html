The law in Ohio

The same 10 questions are asked of every state. Each answer names its source and the date it was checked.

Is it legal here?

Can adults 21 and older use cannabis without a medical card?

Allowed Yes, 21 and older

Adults 21 and older may use and possess marijuana bought from a licensed Ohio dispensary, up to 2.5 ounces of plant material and 15 grams of extract in total, counting homegrown marijuana.

Ohio Revised Code 3796.221 · in effect March 20, 2026 · checked November 5, 2026 [1] Official source

Is there a state medical cannabis program?

Allowed Yes

A patient registered with the Division of Cannabis Control, with a certified physician’s recommendation for a qualifying condition, may possess up to a 90-day supply bought from a licensed dispensary. Caregivers register separately.

Ohio Revised Code 3796.22 and 3796.08 · in effect March 20, 2026 · checked November 5, 2026 [2][3] Official source

Buying and having it

Where can adults buy it legally?

Allowed Licensed Ohio dispensaries

Adult-use marijuana must be bought from a licensed Ohio dispensary.

Ohio Revised Code 3796.221 · in effect March 20, 2026 · checked November 5, 2026 [1] Official source

How much can an adult have?

Allowed 2.5 ounces plant material, 15 g extract

The limit counts dispensary and homegrown marijuana together.

Ohio Revised Code 3796.221 · in effect March 20, 2026 · checked November 5, 2026 [1] Official source

Can adults share or gift it?

Limited Same amounts, free, on private property only

An adult may give the same amounts to another adult in a single day, without payment, only on private residential or agricultural property. Other transfers require a license.

Ohio Revised Code 3796.221 · in effect March 20, 2026 · checked November 5, 2026 [1] Official source

Growing at home

Can adults grow it at home?

Allowed 6 plants per adult, 12 per home

Subject to secured-area, primary-residence and other statutory conditions.

Ohio Revised Code 3796.04 · in effect March 20, 2026 · checked October 9, 2026 [4] Official source

Using it

Can you use it in public?

Not permitted No

Using adult-use or homegrown marijuana in a public place is prohibited. Use is legal at a private residence, subject to the property owner’s rules.

Ohio Revised Code 3796.04 · in effect March 20, 2026 · checked November 5, 2026 [5] Official source

What are the driving rules?

Not permitted No driving under the influence

Neither the adult-use nor the medical law allows driving under the influence. Driving with marijuana or its metabolite at or above set blood or urine concentrations is an offense even without shown impairment, and medical registration is not a defense.

Ohio Revised Code 3796.221 and 4511.19 · in effect March 20, 2026 · checked November 5, 2026 [1][6] Official source

Work and past records

Does state law protect workers who use cannabis off the job?

Labels sum up what the cited state law says about employers and cannabis use away from work. They are not a ruling on any one job, employer or drug test.

Not protected Employers may act on marijuana use

Ohio’s marijuana law does not require employers to allow or accommodate marijuana use, possession or distribution. Employers may refuse to hire, discharge or discipline someone because of marijuana use, and may keep drug-testing, drug-free workplace and zero-tolerance policies. This applies to medical and adult-use marijuana alike.

Ohio Revised Code 3796.28 · in effect March 20, 2026 · checked November 5, 2026 [7] Official source

Can a past cannabis record be cleared?

Labels name the process the cited state law provides. Whether one record qualifies depends on that record.

By application Some possession cases before March 20, 2026

An application-based process to expunge specified marijuana and hashish possession cases from before March 20, 2026. Eligibility and court procedures are stated in the statute.

Ohio Revised Code 2953.321 · in effect March 20, 2026 · checked October 9, 2026 [8] Official source
