The law in Oregon

The same 10 questions are asked of every state. Each answer names its source and the date it was checked.

Is it legal here?

Can adults 21 and older use cannabis without a medical card?

Allowed Yes, 21 and older

Adults 21 and older can possess and use cannabis within set limits.

Oregon Health Authority, Cannabis laws in Oregon · checked November 15, 2026 [1] Official source

Is there a state medical cannabis program?

Allowed Yes

The Oregon Medical Marijuana Act allows medical use for qualifying medical conditions.

Oregon Health Authority, Cannabis laws in Oregon · checked November 15, 2026 [1] Official source

Buying and having it

Where can adults buy it legally?

Allowed OLCC-licensed retailers

Marijuana items may be bought at an OLCC-licensed retail location. A customer may buy up to 2 ounces of usable marijuana at one time or in one day.

Oregon Liquor and Cannabis Commission, Frequently asked questions · checked November 15, 2026 [2] Official source

How much can an adult have?

Allowed 2 ounces in public, 8 at home

Also 16 ounces of solid products or concentrates, 72 ounces of liquid products and 1 ounce of extracts bought from a marijuana retailer.

Oregon Health Authority, Cannabis laws in Oregon · checked November 15, 2026 [1] Official source

Can adults share or gift it?

Allowed Up to 1 ounce, noncommercial

An adult may deliver up to 1 ounce of usable marijuana at a time to another adult 21 or older for noncommercial purposes.

ORS 475C.305(7) (2025 edition) · checked November 15, 2026 [3] Official source

Growing at home

Can adults grow it at home?

Allowed 4 plants

The personal possession limits include four marijuana plants.

Oregon Health Authority, Cannabis laws in Oregon · checked November 15, 2026 [1] Official source

Using it

Can you use it in public?

Not permitted No

Work and past records

Does state law protect workers who use cannabis off the job?

Labels sum up what the cited state law says about employers and cannabis use away from work. They are not a ruling on any one job, employer or drug test.

Not protected Depends on the employer’s policies

The Oregon Liquor and Cannabis Commission says the state’s cannabis law does not change existing employment law. Employers who test for drugs may keep doing so, and whether a person can be fired for using marijuana depends on the employer’s policies.

Oregon Liquor and Cannabis Commission, Frequently asked questions · checked November 15, 2026 [2] Official source

Can a past cannabis record be cleared?

Labels name the process the cited state law provides. Whether one record qualifies depends on that record.

By application Qualifying convictions, no fee or waiting period

Oregon’s courts say ORS 475C.397 requires a court to set aside a qualifying marijuana conviction, with no waiting period and no filing fee, unless the prosecutor objects within 30 days that the conviction does not qualify. On the statewide form a person declares that the conduct took place before July 1, 2015 and that the conviction was for conduct described in ORS 475C.305 or for possessing less than one ounce of dried marijuana. The same form asks the court under ORS 137.222 to lower a conviction for an offense that has since been reduced. A set-aside seals the record and the person is treated as not previously convicted. Separately, on November 21, 2022 the Governor pardoned state-court convictions for possessing one ounce or less in pre-2015 cases where the person was 21 or older, it was the only charge and there was no victim; those records were sealed without an application.

ORS 475C.397 and ORS 137.222, per the Oregon Judicial Department’s marijuana set-aside motion form (July 2023) · checked November 15, 2026 [4][5] Official source
