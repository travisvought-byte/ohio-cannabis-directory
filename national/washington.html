Is weed legal in Washington?

Short answer: Yes. Weed is legal in Washington for adults 21 and older, and for medical patients.

Checked November 15, 2026 against Washington State Liquor and Cannabis Board, Know the law [1]

If you are 21 or older in Washington, here is the short version. Buy: State-licensed retail stores · Carry: 1 oz, 16 oz solid edibles, 72 oz liquid, 7 g conc · Grow at home: No, except medical patients. Old cannabis record holding you back? In Washington: Misdemeanors at age 21 or older.

Adult use: Allowed Yes, 21 and older
Medical use: Allowed Yes, with a practitioner’s authorization

Official state site: Know the law (Washington State Liquor and Cannabis Board, checked November 15, 2026)

How patients get a medical card in Washington

From Washington State Department of Health. Adults 21 and older can also buy without a card; see the answers below.

Who can get one
Any patient, regardless of age, whose healthcare practitioner authorizes cannabis as medically appropriate. Minors need a parent or legal guardian as their designated provider. [2]
Cost
At least $1 for the recognition card. Stores may charge more. [2]
How long it lasts
An authorization can be good for up to a year for an adult and up to six months for a minor. [2]
Caregivers
A designated provider 21 or older, with their own authorization form from the patient’s practitioner, can buy and grow for the patient. [2]
Where patients buy
Medically endorsed retail stores. Patients with a recognition card pay no excise or sales tax on Department of Health medical products there, and may hold up to three times the recreational limits. [2]

Not stated on the state pages we checked: visiting patients. Check with the state.

The law in Washington

The same 10 questions are asked of every state. Each answer names its source and the date it was checked.

Is it legal here?

Can adults 21 and older use cannabis without a medical card?

Allowed Yes, 21 and older

Only adults 21 and older can buy and possess cannabis.

Washington State Liquor and Cannabis Board, Know the law · checked November 15, 2026 [1] Official source

Is there a state medical cannabis program?

Allowed Yes, with a practitioner’s authorization

With a health care practitioner’s authorization, an adult patient may grow up to four plants. A recognition card from a medically endorsed store adds tax exemptions and higher limits: by default up to 3 ounces of usable cannabis (or equivalent products) and six plants. Patients 18 to 20 must register to buy.

Washington State Department of Health; RCW 69.51A.210 · checked November 15, 2026 [3][4] Official source

Buying and having it

Where can adults buy it legally?

Allowed State-licensed retail stores

Cannabis can only be sold and bought at state-licensed retail stores, with valid photo ID. Adults may buy up to 1 ounce of usable cannabis, 16 ounces of solid edibles, 72 ounces in liquid form and 7 grams of concentrates.

Washington State Liquor and Cannabis Board, Know the law · checked November 15, 2026 [1] Official source

How much can an adult have?

Allowed 1 oz, 16 oz solid edibles, 72 oz liquid, 7 g conc.

Adults 21 and older may buy and possess up to 1 ounce of usable cannabis, 16 ounces of solid edibles, 72 ounces of drinks and 7 grams of concentrate.

Washington State Liquor and Cannabis Board, Using and having cannabis · checked November 15, 2026 [5] Official source

Can adults share or gift it?

Allowed Up to 1/2 oz flower a day, free

In any 24-hour period, an adult may give another adult 21 or older up to half an ounce of usable cannabis or 8 ounces of solid edibles, with separate limits for other products, with no payment involved. The handoff must take place out of public view in a nonpublic place, or the product must be in its original store packaging.

RCW 69.50.4013(4), as reprinted in 2026 Senate Bill 6204; House bill report on HB 1449 (2025) · checked November 15, 2026 [6][7] Official source

Growing at home

Can adults grow it at home?

Not permitted No, except medical patients

Washington does not allow home growing unless you are a qualified medical patient or designated provider with a valid authorization. Growing plants without one is a class C felony; a 2026 bill to allow adult home grows did not pass.

Washington State Liquor and Cannabis Board, Growing cannabis at home; House bill report on HB 1449 (2025); SB 6204 bill summary · checked November 15, 2026 [8][7][9] Official source

Using it

Can you use it in public?

Not permitted No

It is illegal to consume cannabis in view of the public.

Washington State Liquor and Cannabis Board, Know the law · checked November 15, 2026 [1] Official source

What are the driving rules?

Not permitted Illegal above 5 ng/ml THC

Driving with more than 5 nanograms of active THC per milliliter of blood is illegal.

Washington State Liquor and Cannabis Board, Know the law · checked November 15, 2026 [1] Official source

Work and past records

Does state law protect workers who use cannabis off the job?

Labels sum up what the cited state law says about employers and cannabis use away from work. They are not a ruling on any one job, employer or drug test.

Partly protected Job applicants only

Since January 1, 2024, it is unlawful for an employer to discriminate in initial hiring because of a person’s use of cannabis off the job and away from the workplace, or because an employer-required drug test finds non-psychoactive cannabis metabolites. The law covers initial hiring only: it does not apply to testing after hiring, such as post-accident or suspicion-based testing, and it leaves drug-free workplace rules in place. It does not cover applicants for jobs that need a federal background investigation or security clearance, jobs with law enforcement agencies and fire departments, other first responders, corrections officers, airline and aerospace jobs, or safety-sensitive jobs the employer identified in advance.

RCW 49.44.240 · in effect January 1, 2024 · checked November 15, 2026 [10] Official source

Can a past cannabis record be cleared?

Labels name the process the cited state law provides. Whether one record qualifies depends on that record.

By application Misdemeanors at age 21 or older

Washington’s courts say the law does not vacate a conviction automatically: a person files a petition (form CrRLJ 09.0800) under RCW 9.96.060, gives the prosecutor a copy the same day and attends a hearing. A court will vacate a misdemeanor cannabis conviction if the person was 21 or older at the time of the offense; eligible offenses include those under RCW 69.50.4014 from July 1, 2004, under RCW 69.50.401(e) or (d) from May 21, 1971 to July 1, 2004, and under equivalent local ordinances. After vacation a person may state on job or housing applications that they were never convicted of that crime.

RCW 9.96.060, per the Washington Courts’ Instructions for Vacating Misdemeanor and Gross Misdemeanor Convictions (CrRLJ 09.0300, July 2022) · checked November 15, 2026 [11] Official source
