Is it legal here?

Can adults 21 and older use cannabis without a medical card?

Not permitted No

Kansas has no adult-use law. Possessing marijuana is a Class B nonperson misdemeanor. One prior conviction makes it a Class A misdemeanor, and two or more make it a drug felony.

K.S.A. 21-5706(c)(3) · checked November 15, 2026 [1] Official source

Is there a state medical cannabis program?

Limited Legal defense for CBD treatment oil

Kansas law gives an affirmative defense to a person charged with possessing a cannabidiol treatment preparation for a debilitating medical condition who has a letter from a Kansas physician dated within the past 15 months. It is a defense in court, not a program for buying medical marijuana.

K.S.A. 21-5706(d) · checked November 15, 2026 [1] Official source
