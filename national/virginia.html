How patients get a medical card in Virginia

From Virginia Cannabis Control Authority. Adults 21 and older can also buy without a card; see the answers below.

Steps

  1. Meet the basic qualifications.
  2. See an eligible practitioner: a physician, physician assistant or advanced practice registered nurse. If you are a good candidate, they issue a written certification.
  3. Take the certification and your government-issued ID to a licensed dispensary, which scans the certification’s QR code.

Steps as the state describes them [1]

Who can get one
Permanent or temporary Virginia residents, 18 or older or with a parent or guardian’s consent, with a valid government-issued ID and a condition a licensed practitioner finds may benefit. [1]
How long it lasts
The practitioner sets how long the written certification lasts, up to one year. [1]
Caregivers
The practitioner can add a registered agent to the certification for patients who need help. Parents or guardians can receive a certification for a minor or vulnerable adult. [1]
Where patients buy
Licensed medical cannabis dispensaries. [1]

Is it legal here?

Can adults 21 and older use cannabis without a medical card?

Allowed Yes, 21 and older

Adults 21 and older may possess up to 2 ounces outside their home and generally may use cannabis in private residences.

Virginia Cannabis Control Authority, Frequently asked questions · checked November 15, 2026 [2] Official source

Is there a state medical cannabis program?

Allowed Yes

Virginia runs a medical cannabis program.

Virginia Cannabis Control Authority · checked November 15, 2026 [3] Official source

Buying and having it

Where can adults buy it legally?

Not permitted Not until July 1, 2027

Non-medical sales are currently illegal. Retail sales begin July 1, 2027.

Virginia Cannabis Control Authority · checked November 15, 2026 [2][3] Official source

How much can an adult have?

Allowed 2 ounces outside the home

Adults 21 and older may possess up to 2 ounces outside their private residence.

Virginia Cannabis Control Authority, Frequently asked questions · checked November 15, 2026 [2] Official source

Can adults share or gift it?

Allowed Up to 2 ounces, free

Adults 21 and older may transfer 2 ounces or less to another adult without payment. Gifts tied to a sale or another exchange do not count.

Virginia Cannabis Control Authority, Frequently asked questions · checked November 15, 2026 [2] Official source

Growing at home

Can adults grow it at home?

Allowed Up to 4 plants per household

Four plants per household, not per person, under specified requirements.

Virginia Cannabis Control Authority, Frequently asked questions · checked November 15, 2026 [2] Official source

Using it

Can you use it in public?

Not permitted No

Consuming cannabis in any public place is illegal, as is using it while driving or riding as a passenger.

Virginia Cannabis Control Authority, Frequently asked questions · checked November 15, 2026 [2] Official source

What are the driving rules?

Not permitted No impaired driving or use in a vehicle

It is illegal to drive under the influence of marijuana, or to use it while driving or riding as a passenger.

Virginia Cannabis Control Authority, Frequently asked questions · checked November 15, 2026 [2] Official source

Work and past records

Does state law protect workers who use cannabis off the job?

Labels sum up what the cited state law says about employers and cannabis use away from work. They are not a ruling on any one job, employer or drug test.

Partly protected Some medical cannabis use only

Virginia’s Cannabis Control Authority says workplaces and employers may still prohibit cannabis use by their employees and enforce drug testing, and that Virginia law protects some medical cannabis use from employment action, though perhaps not for every medical user. The Marijuana Policy Project, an advocacy group, lists that protection as covering employees who use cannabis oil under a valid written certification, with exceptions for impairment or possession at work, federal law and some defense-industry employers (Va. Code § 40.1-27.4); that part is not yet checked against the state’s own law.

Virginia Cannabis Control Authority, Frequently asked questions; Va. Code § 40.1-27.4, per the Marijuana Policy Project’s chart · checked November 15, 2026 [2][4] Official source and national compilation

Can a past cannabis record be cleared?

Labels name the process the cited state law provides. Whether one record qualifies depends on that record.

Automatic Possession offenses, criminal and civil

Virginia State Police say statutes taking effect July 1, 2026 (Va. Code § 19.2-392.5 and following) seal possession of marijuana offenses, criminal and civil, automatically, along with some other records; the courts’ July 2026 guide refers to former § 18.2-250.1 possession cases as automatically sealed under § 19.2-392.6:1. A no-fee court petition covers records that should have been sealed automatically but were not, offenses the system cannot identify, such as possession of marijuana-related drug paraphernalia, and misdemeanor sale or distribution of marijuana with an offense date on or after January 1, 1986. Other convictions can be sealed by petition after seven years for a misdemeanor or ten for a felony, with further conditions. Background check companies may not share sealed records.

Va. Code § 19.2-392.5 and following, per the Virginia State Police’s Sealing of Criminal Records page and the courts’ sealing guide CC-1203 INST (07/26) · in effect July 1, 2026 · checked November 15, 2026 [5][6] Official source
