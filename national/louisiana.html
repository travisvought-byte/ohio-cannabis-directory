Adult use: Medical patients only No

How patients get a medical card in Louisiana

From Louisiana Department of Health. Adults without a card cannot buy here.

Who can get one
Any patient with a valid recommendation from an authorized Louisiana clinician in good standing with their licensing board. No state agency keeps a list of recommending clinicians. [1]
How long it lasts
A recommendation expires no more than 12 months after it is issued. [1]
Where patients buy
Licensed retailers: up to 10 base sites, each with up to two satellite locations. Raw flower is limited to 71 grams every 14 days, and retailers may not deliver outside Louisiana. [1]

The law in Louisiana

The same 10 questions are asked of every state. Each answer names its source and the date it was checked.

Is it legal here?

Can adults 21 and older use cannabis without a medical card?

Medical patients only No

Louisiana has no adult-use law. Without medical authorization, possessing 14 grams or less brings a summons and a fine of up to $100. A first conviction for more than 14 grams can bring up to six months in jail, a $500 fine, or both.

La. R.S. 40:966 · checked November 15, 2026 [3] Official source

Is there a state medical cannabis program?

Allowed Yes, with a clinician’s recommendation

The Louisiana Department of Health regulates medical marijuana. A patient with a recommendation from an authorized clinician buys from licensed retailers, and raw flower is limited to 71 grams (2.5 ounces) every 14 days. State law exempts patients in the program from the possession penalties.

Louisiana Department of Health; La. R.S. 40:966(F) · checked November 15, 2026 [4][3] Official source

Work and past records

Does state law protect workers who use cannabis off the job?

Labels sum up what the cited state law says about employers and cannabis use away from work. They are not a ruling on any one job, employer or drug test.

Partly protected State employees who are medical patients

Louisiana law bars a state employer from penalizing an employee or job applicant solely for a positive marijuana test if the person has a clinically diagnosed debilitating medical condition and a licensed physician has recommended marijuana under the state’s medical marijuana law. A state employer may still act against an employee who uses or is impaired by marijuana at work or during work hours, or whose main job is to drive or maintain state vehicles or supervise those who do, and the rule does not cover emergency medical services, law enforcement, public safety officials, firefighters or the horse racing commission. The section covers only state employers; a 2024 bill to extend it to city, parish and municipal employers was not checked.

La. R.S. 49:1016 (Acts 2022, No. 651) · checked November 15, 2026 [5] Official source

Can a past cannabis record be cleared?

Labels name the process the cited state law provides. Whether one record qualifies depends on that record.

By application First marijuana possession: motion after 90 days

Louisiana courts expunge a misdemeanor conviction on motion. A first-offense misdemeanor conviction for possessing marijuana, THC or its derivatives can be expunged once 90 days have passed since the conviction (C.Cr.P. art. 977(D)). Other misdemeanor convictions qualify once set aside under Article 894(B), or five years after the sentence ends with no felony conviction or pending felony charge, as certified by the district attorney; some offenses, such as domestic abuse battery and stalking, are excluded. The total cost is capped at $550, with fee exemptions such as for some dismissed cases; a lower $300 cap for first-offense marijuana possession (art. 983(M)) ended on August 1, 2026 under its own terms. Felony convictions follow separate rules.

La. C.Cr.P. arts. 977 and 983 · checked November 16, 2026 [6][7] Official source
