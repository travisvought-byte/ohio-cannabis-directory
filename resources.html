Marijuana-specific expungement: ORC 2953.321

Effective March 20, 2026. Covers specified marijuana/hashish possession cases from before that date, including certain dismissed cases. Apply to the sentencing court with case information and evidence of the covered offense. Relief requires a court process; it is not automatic. The statute sets a $50 application fee unless indigent.

Understand the Ohio rules

Home growing: ORC 3796.04

Adults 21+ may grow up to six plants per person, with no more than twelve at one residence. Grow at your primary residence in a secured enclosed area, inaccessible to people under 21 and not visible from public space. Rental prohibitions and other residence restrictions apply. The section prohibits selling homegrown marijuana, hydrocarbon extraction and public consumption.
