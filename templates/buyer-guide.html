New to cannabis? Make a short shopping plan, then show it to the person helping you at the dispensary.

What I want help with

You can say this

“I'm new to this. Please help me find something easy to use. I'd like a small first purchase, and I need you to explain the label.”

Ask before you buy

  • How do I use and measure this?
  • How long before it starts working, and how long should I wait before using more?
  • What is the total price? Do I need anything else to use it?

Tell them your experience and budget. It's fine to say “I don't know” or leave without buying.

Already bought something? See how long it will last.

Example: 5 packages × 10 gummies = 50 gummies. At 1 a day, that's 50 days.

Fill in the three numbers to see how long your supply will last.

Counts your supply using the amount you enter. It does not tell you how much to use.

Before you go

Bring a valid photo ID. If you are a medical patient, bring your active registry card. Check the dispensary's payment options before leaving.

If you take medicines or have health concerns, ask a clinician or pharmacist about cannabis first. Don't drive impaired. Keep products locked away from children and pets. Avoid combining with alcohol.
