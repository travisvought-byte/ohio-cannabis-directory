Grassroots to Statehouse: Ohio Cannabis Policy Weekend

Two hands-on days on how Ohio cannabis law actually gets made, and how patients, veterans, advocates and the people who work in this industry can have a real say in it. Classes, practice sessions and panels with people who do this work for a living.

  • Sat and Sun, January 16-17, 2027 (planned)
  • Crowne Plaza Columbus North-Worthington (planned)
  • Open to everyone, every party and none

Why this weekend

Ohio voters legalized adult-use cannabis in 2023, and lawmakers have been rewriting that law ever since. Most people affected by those changes, from patients to shop owners, never learn where the decisions happen or how to get a word in before a vote. This weekend is about closing that gap: who decides, when you can speak up, and how to do it well.

What you'll learn

  • How a bill becomes law in Ohio

    Committees, hearings, floor votes and the governor's desk, using a recent cannabis bill as the case study.

  • Testifying at the Statehouse

    How to sign up, what to put in written testimony, and five minutes that a committee will remember. Includes a practice hearing.

  • Lobbying 101

    Meeting your own legislator, building a coalition, and who does and doesn't have to register as a lobbyist in Ohio.

  • Running for local office

    Council, township and school board: petitions, filing deadlines, and what a first campaign really takes.

  • Working with regulators

    How state cannabis rules are written and how to comment on them before they take effect.

  • Local zoning and city hall

    Where your town stands on dispensaries, and how residents and businesses shape local ordinances.

  • Patients and veterans panel

    Access, cost and medical rights, told by the people living with the rules.

  • Industry panel

    Operators, attorneys and advisors on what the law changes mean for business and how the industry makes its case.

Questions

Is this a political party event?
No. It's civic education, open to people of every party and none, and we'll invite speakers from across the spectrum. The Libertarian Party of Ohio is planning its own gathering at the same location that weekend; it's a separate event with its own program.
Will there be cannabis on site?
No. No cannabis sales, use, sampling, giveaways or product display anywhere on the property. This is a learning event, and the hotel's rules are firm on it.
Who is it for?
Patients and caregivers, veterans, advocates, students, and anyone who works in or around Ohio's cannabis industry. All ages are welcome.
What will it cost?
Not set yet. Our aim is a low-cost ticket for attendees, with sponsors covering most of the room. We'll post prices before registration opens.
Is the venue accessible?
The hotel lists itself as wheelchair accessible with free parking. We'll confirm step-free access to every session room before we open registration. Tell us what you need, from seating to captioning, and we'll plan for it.
Can I get the slides or recordings?
We plan to post class handouts and a "how to testify" guide on this page after the weekend.
