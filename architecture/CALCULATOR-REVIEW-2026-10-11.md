# Supply calculator source review

Reviewed October 11, 2026.

The calculator estimates inventory duration, using amount purchased divided by the visitor’s own daily total. It does not set a typical consumption amount or infer a dose from product, purpose or consumption method. The optional helper multiplies an entered amount per use by entered uses per day.

NIST SP 1020 Table 9 confirms one avoirdupois ounce is exactly 28.349523125 grams. Duration is computed with that factor and displayed approximately in days and weeks. Visitors can enter the labeled gram weight instead.
https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/SpecialPublications/NIST.SP.1020.pdf

Leaf411 publishes purchase-frequency and flower-quantity examples as part of broader cannabinoid arithmetic. This supplies a comparable resource; its dose calculations and rounded fraction conversions were not copied.
https://library.leaf411.org/hc/en-us/articles/14014920751127-Cannabis-Calculator-resources

Health Canada describes variation by individual, frequency, product and other factors, and distinguishes ingestion from inhalation. No universal joint-to-pipe or bong conversion is assumed here.
https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/drugs-medication/cannabis/resources/what-you-need-to-know-if-you-choose-to-consume-cannabis.html
