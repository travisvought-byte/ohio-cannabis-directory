## Growing at home

Adults 21+ may grow up to 6 plants each, and no more than 12 at a single residence, at their primary residence, subject to the security and other conditions in [R.C. 3796.04](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-3796.04). The home-grow section authorizes storage at the primary residence and prohibits sale or profit. The transportation section separately permits transporting homegrown cannabis under its vehicle-storage conditions ([R.C. 3796.062(C)](https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-3796.062)).
