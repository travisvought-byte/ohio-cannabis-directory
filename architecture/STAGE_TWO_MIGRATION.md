# Stage two: canonical Ohio data and shared state renderer

Completed October 9, 2026. This stage migrates the existing dataset; it does not claim complete statewide market coverage.

## Source of truth

`data/directory.json` is the canonical directory. The old organization CSV, relationship CSV and seed JSON are compatibility downloads generated by `export_directory.py`. Existing Ohio builders use adapter functions in `directory_data.py` to read the canonical records. Keep updates in canonical data, then regenerate and validate. Do not edit generated downloads or HTML as the source of truth.

The original 564 CSV records, 518 public records, 46 retained records, all record IDs and all 60 relationship IDs are preserved. Original fields and provenance remain in `legacy_record`. Seed/supply inputs are retained as `legacy_seed_record`, and original download bytes were preserved during migration. `data/migration-report.json` records baseline hashes and scope.

The structured organization total includes additional seed providers, consumer referral providers and relationship-only counterparties. These are not announced as newly verified businesses. Reference-only counterparties appear in relationship references and do not become standalone service listings. A provider offering multiple referral routes uses one organization identity. Indoor Gardens' seed pilot identity merges into its original CSV identity through an alias.

## Shared state presentation

`templates/state.html` and `build_state.py` render state packs and services using one template. The public `state.html` page offers all seven need-based pathways. It preserves shareable state/pathway/search URLs and permanent service links. Business cards link back to existing `index.html?org=...` IDs. Providers without legacy organization cards expose their stable service link rather than a broken original-directory link.

The renderer includes only live/pilot states. Unknown state requests display an unpublished-state message with no fallback Ohio results. Pilot states are labeled. The Pennsylvania pilot uses the shared renderer for 148 source-reviewed resource routes across seven pathways and six cited law summaries, with per-pathway coverage limits. The October 28 publication adds 76 public-source routes in business services, education, record relief and patient/community support. Historical or unresolved research does not establish current service intake. Synthetic Washington fixtures exercise the research-only publication gate; they are not researched Washington data. The physical finder includes 200 entries from the September 16, 2026 PA regulator roster; legal-aid offices and supplier mailing addresses do not become dispensaries.

Seed sex and flowering behavior can be filtered independently in both the seed page and shared state page. Supplier filters describe reviewed catalog categories, not a guarantee that every selected combination is sold. Shared-state shipping filters require claim-specific evidence, distinguish seller statements from manufacturer listings, and do not imply lawful delivery. Physical shop evidence is distinguished from Ohio seed pickup. Active filter chips can be removed individually.

The community view hides expired events and displays imported event dates as historical listings requiring confirmation. Existing consumer pages and offline directory/intake workflows remain available.

## Evidence and location limits

Historical profiles have `source-listed` evidence, preserving any original review date. Migration never invents a fresh review date when one is absent. Newly decomposed fulfillment evidence comes from the already reviewed provider policy or manufacturer listing; no test order or new direct confirmation is implied.

Structured coverage remains empty where original service-area descriptions lack claim-level proof. The original text is preserved and labeled as historical. Editorial `listed_in: ["OH"]` means an entry belongs to the Ohio directory, not that its service covers all Ohio residents. A national provider's physical address is also separate from shipping/service coverage.

No physical location rows were created from inferred addresses. The official Ohio location lookup remains available through the dispensary pathway. Physical finder snapshots and their explicit location/geocoding limits are maintained separately from service inquiries. Legal summaries link to official sources; effective dates are retained in structured rules and existing consumer summaries.

## Update and build

1. Edit the relevant entity/service/evidence/state records in `data/directory.json`. Reuse existing organization IDs and create aliases for merges.
2. Preserve original fields in the legacy adapters while adding stronger structured claims. Claim-specific evidence is required before adding confirmed fulfillment or coverage.
3. Run `python validate_foundation.py data/directory.json`.
4. Run `python export_directory.py`.
5. Run all public builders: `build_directory.py`, `build_intake.py`, `build_relationships.py`, `build_resources.py`, `build_seeds.py`, `build_state.py`.
6. Run `python tests/check_data.py`, `python tests/foundation.py`, `python tests/migration.py`, `npm test`, and `npm run test:browser`.

GitHub CI checks the canonical schema, migration/compatibility exports, reference integrity, publication gates, generated pages and browser/mobile behavior.

## Pennsylvania company directory

`build_pa_companies.py` and `templates/pa-companies.html` render a company-focused view from public canonical PA business-service records. The 94 cards group by permanent organization ID; another service for an existing organization does not create another company. Government starting-point routes stay in the resource portal. Existing national organization identities are reused. No Ohio legacy contact or service-area field becomes a newly reviewed PA fact.

Canonical organization evidence with `company-` and `public-business-` claims supplies source-dated people, business emails/phones, brands, affiliations and PA connections. `company-role` and `company-category` are directory classifications of the source-described activities, not credentials or permits. Other existing business routes use conservative editorial categories inferred from their published service descriptions. A brand relationship, local office, provider-advertised reach and active permit remain separate claims.

Run `python build_pa_companies.py` after canonical updates. It generates `pennsylvania-directory.html`, `data/public-pa-companies.json` and `pennsylvania-companies.csv`. Run `python tests/pa_companies.py` and the company browser suite in addition to the normal validation pipeline. Source review dates are retained independently of the directory publication date. Research queues, failed retrieval logs and internal import files remain in Drive staging.
