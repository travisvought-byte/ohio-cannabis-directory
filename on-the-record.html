Profile: Ohio Cannabis Coalition (OHCANN)

Built from the same kinds of record as the rest of this page: its own website and statements, its lobbying registration, the committee records and its tax returns. Its directory listing keeps record ID org-28996af4250a.

What it says it is
“As the Ohio Cannabis Coalition, we represent licensed cultivators, processors, dispensaries, testing laboratories and ancillary businesses that operate in Ohio’s cannabis industry.” Source
Tax status
501(c)(6) business league, per ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer (IRS data), EIN 83-3732401. Its Form 990 filings
Mission on its 2024 return, as filed
“OHCANN WAS FORMED TO ADVOCATE AND EDUCATE ON BEHALF OF OUR MEMBERS. WE WORK COLLABORATIVELY WITH THE REGULATORS AND THE LEGISLATURE TO ENHANCE AND IMPROVE THE OHIO CANNABIS INDUSTRY TO ENSURE OHIOANS HAVE CONSISTENT ACCESS TO A SAFE SUPPLY OF CANNABIS.”
Membership benefit it lists
“Face-to-face access to legislators at Lobby Day events.” Source
Staff on its website
David Bowling (Executive Director), Emily Graham (Deputy Executive Director), Dave Hannan (Director of Finance) Source

Andy Rayburn (Board President; CEO, Buckeye Relief), Adam Thomarios (Board Secretary; CEO, Klutch Cannabis), Daniel Kessler (Board Treasurer; CEO, Riviera Creek), Ray Boyer (Board Member; Partner, Rooted Management Services), Jared Maloof (Board Member; CEO, Standard Wellness), Bill Williams (Board Member; CEO, BeneLeaves)

Former and alternate names

  • National Cannabis Industry Association of Ohio (NCIAO): “OHCANN was originally established in 2017 as the National Cannabis Industry Association of Ohio (NCIAO) – an NCIA affiliate.” Source
  • Ohio Medical Cannabis Industry Association (OMCIA): “In 2019 the association evolved to serve as the voice of Ohio’s growing medical marijuana industry by maintaining a focus on statewide issues impacting our members as Ohio Medical Cannabis Industry Association (OMCIA).” Source
  • Alternate names on its lobbying employer registration: OMCIA, Ohio Medical Cannabis Cultivators Association, OMCCA, Ohio Medical Cannabis Industry Association Source
  • Its tax return for 2023 was filed as Ohio Medical Cannabis Industry Association; the 2024 and 2025 returns as Ohio Cannabis Coalition, doing business as OHCANN Source
