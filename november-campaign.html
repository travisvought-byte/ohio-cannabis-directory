Veteran Home Guardians
A Voice for All Veterans

Retail partner invitation · November 1–30, 2026

Small change.
A reason to keep showing up.

Own, manage or work at a retail business? Consider a November roundup, top-up or donation campaign for Veteran Home Guardians (VHG). Help sustain regular visits and community support for isolated veterans in nursing homes.

Celebrate the Marine Corps birthday on November 10.
Honor Veterans Day on November 11. Carry that support through the whole month.

Choose a way your store can help

Round up

Invite customers to round their purchase up to the next dollar and donate the difference to VHG.

Top up

Offer an optional $1, $5 or customer-selected donation at checkout.

Store-funded donation

Contribute a fixed amount, match customer gifts, or agree on a donation per sale during the campaign.

Local shops, dispensaries and other retailers are invited to discuss a campaign that fits their business. Customer donations should always be voluntary.

Make November count

VHG organizes regular visits to veterans in nursing homes and connects families and care teams with practical resources. Your campaign can help keep that work going, especially in Morrow and Knox counties, Ohio.

Start with a quick conversation

  1. Contact Travis Vought at VHG. Share your business name, location(s), preferred campaign option and proposed dates.
  2. Agree on the details together. Confirm your checkout setup, donation amount or match, customer-facing wording, and how funds will be tracked and transferred before launching.
  3. Invite customers to participate. Run the campaign throughout November, or discuss dates that work for your store.

Discuss a November campaign

Email: travis@vethomeguard.org

Interested employees: share this invitation with your store manager, owner or community-giving team.
