Examples of project work

Targeted research & market maps

Identify the organizations, service providers, relationships and contact routes relevant to a specific market or decision.

Typical deliverable: Defined-scope CSV or report with source links, verification notes, gaps and prioritized next steps.

Custom searchable directories

Build a practical, mobile-friendly directory for an association, regional program, coalition or company. Useful filters, reliable sourcing, correction workflows and downloadable data.

Research upkeep & improvement

Simple process

  1. Describe the problem. Who needs the resource, where, and what decisions should it support?
  2. Agree on scope. We define sources, deliverables, verification limits, price, timeline and ownership/reuse terms before work starts.
  3. Receive something usable. A sourced report, clean dataset, or tested public/private directory with clear gaps and maintenance options.
