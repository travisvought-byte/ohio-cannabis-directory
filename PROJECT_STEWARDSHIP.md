# Project stewardship, attribution, and reuse

_Last documented: October 11, 2026_

## Who does what

**Creator, compiler, and maintainer:** Travis Vought. The repository's `LICENSE` identifies Travis Vought as the copyright claimant for the covered work, and `CITATION.cff` names him as the dataset author. The Git history records development and revisions.

**Charitable beneficiary and distribution context:** [Veteran Home Guardians (VHG)](https://vethomeguard.org/) uses and shares this free public resource in connection with its community mission. VHG branding and the VHG correction inbox describe that relationship; they do not, by themselves, document an assignment of project intellectual property to VHG. VHG's logo and name remain distinct from the project code and data.

These role descriptions do not determine rights to third-party contributions, bare facts, third-party materials, or any work subject to a separate agreement. They are not a transfer, a licensing contract with VHG, or a change to the nonprofit's governance.

## The public resource stays public

The existing repository `LICENSE` specifies **CC BY 4.0** for covered directory data and **MIT** for covered software. Consult that file for the applicable scope and terms. These licenses allow commercial reuse under their conditions. This document does **not** revoke, narrow, or replace previously granted permissions.

Anyone may continue to use the publicly available site and share its links. Corrections and public-resource suggestions remain welcome through the existing VHG contact channel.

## Separate professional and commercial work

Travis may offer separately scoped services, including:

- Software features, integrations, ongoing maintenance, and training
- Custom business assessments and organizational support

See [independent research and implementation services](work-with-travis.html) for the public project-inquiry path.

Such work is distinct from VHG's charitable programs. Fees, deliverables, support levels, and applicable rights for a commissioned engagement should be identified in that engagement; public access to this directory does not promise free custom work or ongoing support. Nothing here represents that a customer must pay to use already open-licensed materials.

**VHG donations and sponsorships** support the nonprofit's mission. They are not payments for Travis's separate professional services. VHG's charitable activities, listings, and nonprofit branding should not be presented as endorsements of unrelated commercial engagements.

## Evidence and maintenance

The repository history, cited sources, published licenses, and contribution records remain the source of project provenance. Preserve author/contributor credits and the license notices when updating or reusing covered materials. Questions about resource corrections should follow the public README; any separate service engagement requires its own scope and communication channel.
