Business financial access & compliance self-assessment

Travis Vought's 33-question diagnostic for Ohio cannabis businesses preparing for banking, financing or internal review.

Imported from your v2 workbook, dated August 27, 2026. Its regulatory references have not been revalidated for this October edition. This measures documented operational readiness; it does not certify compliance or guarantee bank acceptance.

Score what you can demonstrate today. Start with weight-3 questions scored zero, then other zero scores and critical partial controls. Answers stay in this page's memory and disappear on reload; export before leaving. No answers or notes are sent to analytics.

OL-01 · Ownership & Licensing

Do legal entity names, trade names, ownership records, licenses, tax registrations, and banking records consistently identify the same operating structure?

Evidence: Entity documents, state licenses, EIN/tax records, bank account titles · Weight 3

OL-02 · Ownership & Licensing

Is the current ownership list/cap table (or equivalent ownership record) accurate and complete, including direct and indirect owners, financial interests, control relationships, and identification of any person meeting Ohio's 10%-or-greater ownership disclosure threshold (O.A.C. 1301:18-3-04)?

Evidence: Ownership list and capitalization table, organizational chart, 10%+ owner certifications, change logs · Weight 3

OL-03 · Ownership & Licensing

Before entering or completing ownership, financial-interest, control, entity-name, or trade-name changes, does the operator identify applicable DCC notice or prior-approval requirements (O.A.C. 1301:18-3-06 — prior approval required for ownership changes of 10% or more, aggregated per calendar year) and retain evidence of submission or approval?

Evidence: DCC change-of-ownership application, approval/notice correspondence, internal change-control log tracking aggregate ownership movement year-to-date · Weight 3

OL-04 · Ownership & Licensing

Can the company document source of initial and material subsequent capital, loans, and investor funds?

Evidence: Subscription agreements, loan documents, wire records, investor files · Weight 2

OL-05 · Ownership & Licensing

Is there a documented process to keep licenses, permits, registrations, and required ownership disclosures current?

Evidence: License calendar, renewal files, responsible owner, filing evidence · Weight 2

BF-01 · Banking & Financial Access

Does each financial institution used by the company know the nature of the cannabis-related business and permit the relationship?

Evidence: Account agreements, onboarding correspondence, cannabis program documentation · Weight 3

BF-02 · Banking & Financial Access

Are all operating, payroll, tax, merchant, lending, and investment accounts inventoried with clear ownership and authorized signers?

Evidence: Account inventory, signer list, treasury map · Weight 2

BF-03 · Banking & Financial Access

Does the company have a process for responding promptly and consistently to bank due-diligence requests?

Evidence: CDD request log, document package, designated bank liaison · Weight 3

BF-04 · Banking & Financial Access

Are bank-imposed conditions, deposit requirements, transaction restrictions, reporting requirements, and fees documented and monitored?

Evidence: Bank program terms, compliance calendar, exception log · Weight 2

BF-05 · Banking & Financial Access

Is there a contingency plan for account closure, payment-service disruption, or loss of a key banking relationship?

Evidence: Business continuity plan, alternate banking strategy, escalation contacts · Weight 2

CP-01 · Cash & Payments

Are cash receipts, drawers, vaults, transfers, deposits, and variances reconciled under documented procedures?

Evidence: Cash SOP, daily reconciliation, variance reports, deposit logs · Weight 3

CP-02 · Cash & Payments

Are dual control, access restrictions, segregation of duties, and physical-security controls used for material cash movements?

Evidence: Vault access, permissions, dual-count records, camera/security procedures · Weight 3

CP-03 · Cash & Payments

Are armored-car, cash-vault, merchant, ATM, ACH, debit, and other payment providers approved and monitored before use?

Evidence: Contracts, due diligence, fee/flow review, provider inventory · Weight 2

CP-04 · Cash & Payments

Can the company trace material incoming and outgoing funds to a legitimate business purpose and supporting documentation?

Evidence: Invoices, contracts, purchase orders, payment approvals, GL support · Weight 3

AK-01 · AML / KYC / Sanctions

Are owners, control persons, material investors, lenders, and other high-risk counterparties identified and subject to risk-based due diligence?

Evidence: Identity documents, beneficial ownership, background checks, risk files · Weight 3

AK-02 · AML / KYC / Sanctions

Does the company have a risk-based process for sanctions screening of relevant owners, control persons, counterparties, and vendors?

Evidence: Screening procedure, vendor/tool output, escalation records · Weight 3

AK-03 · AML / KYC / Sanctions

Are financial-crime red flags documented for staff, including unusual payments, third-party funding, unexplained cash, structuring indicators, fraud, or identity concerns?

Evidence: Red-flag guide, SOP, training material, escalation examples · Weight 2

AK-04 · AML / KYC / Sanctions

Is there a documented internal escalation path for suspected fraud, illicit finance, sanctions concerns, identity issues, or suspicious counterparties?

Evidence: Escalation matrix, case log, decision records, responsible officer · Weight 3

AK-05 · AML / KYC / Sanctions

Can the company explain its business model, revenue sources, major counterparties, and expected transaction patterns in a way a bank can readily understand?

Evidence: Business narrative, flow-of-funds map, revenue analysis, counterparty profile · Weight 3

VT-01 · Vendor & Third-Party Risk

Are material vendors and service providers subject to documented onboarding and risk-based due diligence?

Evidence: Vendor questionnaire, ownership/licensing checks, contract review · Weight 2

VT-02 · Vendor & Third-Party Risk

Are high-risk vendors, management companies, consultants, payment intermediaries, and related parties identified for enhanced review?

Evidence: Risk tiering, related-party register, enhanced review records · Weight 3

VT-03 · Vendor & Third-Party Risk

Do contracts give the company adequate documentation, audit, termination, compliance, and data-access rights for material providers?

Evidence: Vendor contracts, compliance clauses, audit/data rights · Weight 2

RR-01 · Reconciliation, Tax & Records

Are inventory/seed-to-sale records, point-of-sale data, deposits, and general-ledger revenue periodically reconciled with documented exceptions?

Evidence: METRC/seed-to-sale reports, POS reports, physical inventory/cycle-count records, deposit records, GL reconciliation · Weight 3

RR-02 · Reconciliation, Tax & Records

Are tax obligations, filings, payment deadlines, and tax-payment accounts assigned, documented, and monitored?

Evidence: Tax calendar, filing/payment evidence, responsible owner · Weight 3

RR-03 · Reconciliation, Tax & Records

Are records needed for regulators, banks, auditors, tax authorities, and internal investigations retained in an organized and retrievable manner?

Evidence: Retention schedule, shared repository, access controls, retrieval test · Weight 2

GR-01 · Governance & Response

Is compliance responsibility assigned to a named person who has sufficient access, independence, and authority to stop or escalate activity that may violate law, license requirements, banking conditions, or internal policy?

Evidence: Job description/charter defining authority, escalation-without-retaliation policy, examples of exercised authority · Weight 3

GR-02 · Governance & Response

Do relevant employees receive documented training on cash controls, fraud/red flags, banking requirements, regulatory obligations, and escalation procedures?

Evidence: Training curriculum, attendance records, attestations, refresh schedule · Weight 2

GR-03 · Governance & Response

Are compliance issues, control failures, bank concerns, losses, fraud events, and regulatory findings tracked through remediation and closure?

Evidence: Issue log, corrective-action plans, owners/dates, closure evidence · Weight 3

TX-01 · Tax & Financial Treatment

Has the operator documented its federal tax treatment, IRC §280E cost-of-goods-sold methodology, expense allocation approach, supporting records, and tax-advisor rationale sufficient to defend its treatment in an IRS examination?

Evidence: 280E memo/tax position paper, COGS allocation workpapers, tax preparer engagement letter, prior-year return support · Weight 3

BF-06 · Banking & Financial Access

Can management explain cash receipts and deposits as a percentage of revenue, and reconcile that pattern to licensed locations, POS activity, METRC data, and expected peer/business activity?

Evidence: Cash-to-revenue ratio analysis, deposit records, METRC sales reports, peer/regional benchmark reference · Weight 3

BF-07 · Banking & Financial Access

Do owners and finance personnel understand that cannabis banking commonly involves enhanced due diligence, ongoing transaction monitoring, recurring information requests, and bank-side SAR reporting consistent with applicable FinCEN guidance, and is responsibility assigned for responding accurately and promptly?

Evidence: Onboarding/education materials, designated bank-liaison assignment, response-time log; examples may include Marijuana Limited/Priority/Termination SAR terminology under applicable FinCEN guidance · Weight 2

CP-05 · Cash & Payments

Has the operator prohibited or formally risk-approved payment arrangements that could disguise the underlying cannabis transaction — including cashless-ATM structures or merchant-category-code workarounds — from a financial institution or payment network?

Evidence: Payment-provider contracts, merchant category code documentation, internal policy prohibiting/approving disguised-transaction arrangements · Weight 3

VT-04 · Vendor & Third-Party Risk

Are leases, management agreements, loans, and other transactions with owners or related entities fully disclosed, documented, and supported as bona fide, arm's-length business arrangements?

Evidence: Related-party lease/loan agreements, disclosure schedule, comparable-market-rate support, board/ownership approval records · Weight 3
