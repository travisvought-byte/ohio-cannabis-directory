Before you order or travel
  • Confirm the exact strain, seed sex, flowering type and pack size in stock.
  • Ask whether the seller can fulfill your order to your Ohio ZIP code or offers pickup.
  • Check total cost, payment methods, dispatch time and the policy for delivery or order problems.
